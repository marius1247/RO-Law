## Romania relevance
- **Power & industry:** Romanian generators and industrial emitters are **ETS 1 compliance entities** — EUA exposure sits alongside [[Concept — Cost stack producator energie]] (commodity margin, not network tariff).
- **Fuel distributors:** ETS 2 (from 2027) affects upstream fuel suppliers; retail pass-through interacts with national excise and any overlapping carbon instruments.
- **CBAM:** Steel, cement, aluminium, fertilisers — Romanian exporters/importers need CBAM factor × benchmark parity analysis.
- **BioCCS / DACCS:** Links to [[OUG 163-2022 — promovare energie regenerabila (notă)|OUG 163/2022 RED III transposition]] and EU storage hub access (no domestic geological storage in RO — maritime transport dependency).
