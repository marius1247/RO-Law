# Master Index: Regulatory Dispute Resolution & Litigation Matrix

> **Type:** Procedural dispute-resolution MOC
> **Links:** [[Sistemul de drept al RO (overview)]] · [[Ierarhia actelor normative]] · [[Constitutia Romaniei (notă)]]

Mandatory pre-litigation steps, court jurisdictions, and suspension mechanisms across Romanian commercial, tax, administrative, and constitutional law.

## Forum & appeal matrix

| Dispute type | Pre-litigation requirement | Competent forum |
|---|---|---|
| **Tax decision (ANAF)** | Mandatory *contestație fiscală* within **45 days** — [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (notă)\|CPF]] | Administrative courts (Legea 554/2004) |
| **Public tender (SEAP)** | Guarantee deposit (*cauțiune*) — [[Legea 98-2016 — achizitii publice (notă)\|L98/2016]] | CNSC **or** Bucharest Court of Appeal |
| **Unconstitutional statute** | *Excepție de neconstituționalitate* raised in trial — [[Constitutia Romaniei (notă)\|Const.]] | Curtea Constituțională (CCR) |
| **ANRE regulatory order** | Prior administrative appeal where required | Bucharest Court of Appeal — *contencios administrativ* |
| **Employment dismissal** | — (direct court access typical) | Tribunal — *secția litigii de muncă* — [[Legea 53-2003 — codul muncii (notă)]] |
| **CfD contract (beneficiary–OPCOM)** | — | **Mandatory CACI arbitration** — [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)]] |
| **CfD contribution collection** | Contractual / ordinary civil | Ordinary courts — [[ANRE — Ordin 52-2024 Contracte-cadru CfD (notă)]] |

## Suspension & injunction mechanisms

### Tax assessment suspension

- **Basis:** CPF art. 278 + Legea 554/2004 arts. 14–15
- Administrative appeal does **not** automatically suspend enforcement
- Court suspension requires guarantee (*cauțiune*) up to **20%** of contested liability

### Constitutional & EU-law pleas

- **CCR exception:** Challenge laws/OUGs violating fundamental principles — [[Constitutia Romaniei (notă)]]
- **EU primacy:** Request disapplication of conflicting national provisions — Const. art. 148; [[Sistemul de drept al RO (overview)]]

### Administrative act annulment

- **Basis:** Legea 554/2004 — mandatory *plângere prealabilă* for most ministerial/regulatory acts
- **Suspension of execution:** Art. 14 — separate application; court discretion

## Sector-specific notes

- **Energy:** ANRE penalty decisions → Bucharest CA; CfD arbitration split per HG 318 vs. Ordin 52 — see matrix above
- **Competition:** RCC decisions → administrative court; dawn-raid challenges — [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)]]
- **Procurement:** CNSC 10-day window typical — verify current L98/2016 deadlines — [[Legea 98-2016 — achizitii publice (notă)]]
