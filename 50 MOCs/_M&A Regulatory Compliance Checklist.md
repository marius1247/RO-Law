# Master Index: M&A Regulatory & Corporate Compliance Checklist

> **Type:** Cross-disciplinary M&A compliance MOC
> **Links:** [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță]] · [[MOC — Fiscalitate]] · [[Constitutia Romaniei (notă)]]

End-to-end regulatory review pipeline for share purchases, asset deals, and corporate joint ventures in Romania.

## Clearance lifecycle

```
1. Transaction structuring & threshold assessment
   ├─ Competition filing: [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21/1996 art. 15]]
   └─ Below-threshold call-in / sector FDI review

2. Tax & structural audit
   ├─ IMCA (0.5%) & CIT deductibility: [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)]]
   └─ RO e-Factura / e-TVA: [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)]] · [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (notă)]]

3. HR & operational integration
   ├─ No-poach risk: [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21/1996 art. 5]]
   └─ REGES & restructuring: [[Legea 53-2003 — codul muncii (notă)]]

4. Sectoral licensing
   ├─ Public contracts: [[Legea 98-2016 — achizitii publice (notă)]]
   ├─ Listed targets / financial services: [[Legea 208-2015 — piete de capital (notă)]]
   └─ Environmental permits: [[OUG 195-2005 — protectia mediului (notă)]]
```

## Phase 1 — Competition & merger control

- [ ] **Turnover thresholds:** EUR 10M worldwide combined **and** EUR 4M per party in Romania — [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21/1996 (art. 15)]]
- [ ] **Gun-jumping:** No implementation before RCC clearance
- [ ] **Call-in risk:** Below-threshold transactions in sensitive sectors
- [ ] **HR antitrust:** Scan target contracts for no-poach / wage-fixing clauses

## Phase 2 — Tax due diligence

- [ ] **CIT & IMCA:** Historical profit filings; EBITDA 30% interest cap — [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)]]
- [ ] **VAT / e-Factura:** D300 returns vs. electronic transmission logs — [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)|Titlul VII]]
- [ ] **Prescription:** 5-year general / 10-year tax-crime exception — [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (notă)|CPF art. 110]]
- [ ] **Transfer pricing:** Intercompany files for cross-border related-party transactions

## Phase 3 — Employment integration

- [ ] **REGES-ONLINE:** All employees registered before work commencement — [[Legea 53-2003 — codul muncii (notă)]]
- [ ] **Redundancy planning:** Art. 65 *cauză reală și serioasă* documentation if post-close restructuring

## Phase 4 — Sectoral & public law

- [ ] **Public procurement contracts:** Transfer restrictions on active SEAP awards — [[Legea 98-2016 — achizitii publice (notă)]]
- [ ] **BVB / ASF / BNR:** Fit-and-proper approvals; mandatory takeover bid >33% — [[Legea 208-2015 — piete de capital (notă)]]
- [ ] **Environmental:** AIM continuity; historical contamination — [[OUG 195-2005 — protectia mediului (notă)]]
- [ ] **Energy assets:** CfD status; ATR; licences — [[_Energy Investment Due Diligence Index]]
