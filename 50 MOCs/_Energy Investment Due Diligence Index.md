# Master Index: Energy Investment & Development Due Diligence

> **Type:** Cross-disciplinary MOC / RAG hub
> **Links:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · [[Energetică — sinteza sectorului]] · [[30 Tax & Corporate]] via [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)]]

This index maps the legal, regulatory, and financial compliance pipeline for developing, acquiring, or financing utility-scale renewable (RES) and conventional energy assets in Romania.

## 1. Grid access & market integration

- **ATR assessment:** Verify valid *Aviz Tehnic de Racordare* under [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]] and [[ANRE — Regulament racordare EE 2013 (notă)|racordare regulament]].
- **Reinforcement costs:** Check contingent liabilities for *lucrări de întărire* allocated to the project SPV.
- **Environmental clearance:** Confirm *acord de mediu* and **AIM** under [[OUG 195-2005 — protectia mediului (notă)|OUG 195/2005]].

## 2. Revenue model & subsidy compliance

- **CfD auction:** Audit eligibility under [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)|HG 318/2024]] — negative-price exclusion, CE state-aid conditionality, mandatory organised-market sales.
- **CfD contribution invoicing:** Compliance with [[ANRE — Ordin 52-2024 Contracte-cadru CfD (notă)|Ordin ANRE 52/2024]] levy remittance and OPCOM framework contracts.
- **Corporate PPAs:** Evaluate off-exchange bilateral offtake bankability under liberalised trading rules in [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]].

## 3. Financial, tax & structural risks

- **IMCA (turnover minimum tax):** SPVs with turnover >EUR 50M — higher of 16% CIT or 0.5% adjusted turnover (verify current consolidare) → [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)|Codul fiscal Titlul II]].
- **Dividend WHT:** 16% on distributions (2026 regime) — EU parent-subsidiary exemption if conditions met → [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)|Titlul VI]].
- **RO e-Factura:** Energy sales invoices must route through ANAF XML platform → [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (notă)|Titlul VII]] · [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (notă)|CPF]].

## 4. Competition & merger control

- Acquisition of RES SPVs may trigger [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21/1996]] notification — see [[_M&A Regulatory Compliance Checklist]].

## 5. Market access & operating costs

- **PRE / BRP:** Confirm Balancing Agreement with Transelectrica or valid delegation — [[Concept — PRE si responsabilitate de echilibrare]].
- **Balancing markets:** Separate SS (collective), PCE capacity, PE energy, and PRE imbalance — [[Piețe de echilibrare — sinteza]] · [[Concept — Servicii de sistem si tariful SS]] · [[Concept — Decontare dezechilibre 15 minute]].
- **Network cost stack:** Model TG + SS + TD by delimitation voltage — [[Concept — Cost stack producator energie]] · [[Tarife retea — snapshot 2026]].
- **BESS:** Verify [[ANRE — Norme stocare BESS 2025 (notă)|Ord. 56/2025]] exemption scope; model PCE/PE revenue stacking — [[Concept — BESS economics si tarifare stocare]] · [[Concept — BESS revenue stacking]].
- **Curtailment:** PPA + scheme + PRE schedule alignment — [[Concept — Curtailment si redispatch RES]].
- **OPCOM registration:** [[OPCOM — Procedura inregistrare PEE rev 17 (notă)]] · energy communities [[ANRE — Ordin 9-2026 Comunitati energie (notă)]].
- **EUA / carbon exposure:** Thermal generators and industrial emitters are EU ETS 1 compliance entities — model fuel-switching sensitivity and forward hedge book separately from network tariffs → [[MOC — EU Carbon Pricing]] · [[Concept — Fuel switching price si clean spreads]] · CBAM for covered imports/exports → [[Concept — CBAM formula calcul certificate]].
