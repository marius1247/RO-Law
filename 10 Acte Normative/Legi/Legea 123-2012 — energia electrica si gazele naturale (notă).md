# Legea nr. 123/2012 a energiei electrice și a gazelor naturale — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)]] ✅ *complete* · **Regulator organic law:** [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]] · **RES support:** [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|L220/2008]]

> [!info] Currency
> Working consolidare Portal Legislativ id `307058` (versiune_text 2026-02-27), including notes from **OUG 9/2026** (biogas/biomethane definitions and connection). Heavily amended by **OUG 143/2021** (Dir. 2019/944 package) and later acts — treat article numbers as stable only within this consolidare.

## What it is / scope

Romania's principal primary statute for the **electricity** and **natural-gas** sectors, in two titles under one law. It sets the regulatory perimeter for production, storage, transmission, distribution, supply, trading, aggregation and market operation; network access and connection; unbundling; last-resort / universal service; consumer rights; prices and tariffs for regulated activities; and investigation / sanction powers. ANRE is named as competent authority; its organisation sits in [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]].

For a Moldovan reader this is the closest parallel to stacking `Moldovan Law/.../Legea 164-2025 — energia electrica` (EE, Clean Energy Package) with `Moldovan Law/.../Legea 108-2016 — gazele naturale` (gas), with the important difference that Romania folded both fuels into a single 2012 act and then repeatedly grafted fourth-package concepts onto it (especially via OUG 143/2021) rather than replacing the statute wholesale.

## Key provisions

### Electricity — perimeter and actors

- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Domain of Title I: electricity and heat from cogeneration; security, competitiveness, sustainability, affordability. Explicit exclusions (mobile gensets, DC sources not inverter-connected to SEN). Art. 1(1¹) pulls metering groups at user installations into the ANRE perimeter.
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 3|Art. 3]]** — Definitions dictionary for Title I. Post-OUG 143/2021 this is where **agregare**, **agregator independent**, **consumator activ**, **comunitate de energie a cetățenilor**, **stocare**, **consum dispecerizabil** and related Clean Energy Package vocabulary live — the same cast Moldova put into L164/2025 art. 2. Read art. 3 before any licensing or market advice.
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 7|Art. 7]]** (+ **7¹–7⁶**) — ANRE as autonomous regulatory authority; compliance with Reg. (EU) 2019/943; ACER cooperation; complaint handling; offer-comparison tool; dynamic-price monitoring; national supplier-switching IT platform (links to art. 62). Institutional detail → [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]].

### Licensing

- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 10|Art. 10]]** — Categories of establishment authorisations and licences. Licences now expressly cover: generation (+ co-located storage), cogeneration, transmission & balancing, distribution, market operator, supply, **trading**, **aggregation**, and **standalone storage**. Sub-1 MW generation/storage may operate without a licence but still under ANRE rules (art. 10(5)). Establishment authorisation threshold: >1 MW max power injected. Distribution without concession licence exceptions (closed distribution systems, industrial parks, <3 MW, etc.) sit in art. 10(4²).

Practical consequence: storage, aggregation and trading are inside the RO licensing perimeter — same commercial warning as for Moldova under L164/2025.

### Network access, unbundling, FUI, consumers, tariffs

- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 25|Art. 25]]** — Regulated third-party access and connection as **mandatory services** of TSO/DSO (and citizen energy communities managing distribution). Refusal only for SEN security or capacity shortage, must be reasoned; ANRE decides disputes in two months; market-based capacity allocation when firm capacity is scarce (art. 25(2²)); storage characteristics must be considered in solution studies (art. 25(14)).
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 48|Art. 48]]** — Distribution unbundling (legal / organisational / decision-making independence inside a vertically integrated undertaking; compliance programme and agent; branding separation). Exemption for VIUs serving <100,000 connected customers or small/isolated systems (art. 48(3)). Ownership unbundling of assets is **not** required by this article.
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 53|Art. 53]]** — **Furnizor de ultimă instanță (FUI)**: designated by ANRE via competitive selection; covers customers left without a supplier (licence withdrawal or other ANRE-identified gap), not those disconnected for theft/non-payment. Contract forms ANRE-approved; switch to FUI can occur without signature. Distinct from **serviciul universal** (art. 54) — same conceptual split Moldova now makes explicit in L164/2025 between universal-service supplier and last-resort supplier.
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 62|Art. 62]]** — End-customer rights catalogue: network access; active consumer / demand-response participation; supplier choice including EU-registered suppliers; switching (max 21 days, target 24 hours by 2026); dynamic-price contracts; aggregation contracts without supplier consent; complaint and ANRE dispute routes; household / SME switching without fee (with limited early-termination fee rules for fixed-term contracts).
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 75|Art. 75]]** — Price/tariff typology: regulated for transmission (incl. system services), distribution, connection, and certain ancillary activities; negotiated / market prices for electricity and associated system services. Methodologies → arts. 76, 79; intervention in retail prices tightly constrained by art. 80 (energy-poverty / vulnerable customers; EC notification).

### Gas — definitions and connection

- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 100|Art. 100]]** — Gas Title definitions: TPA to upstream pipelines, distribution, storage, transmission, LNG/hydrogen terminals; capacity products (firm / interruptible / unused); customer categories; vulnerable consumer; codes; biogas / **biometan** (as amended by OUG 9/2026 — ANRE quality conditions within 120 days). Cross-ref to L220/2008 for biomass.
- **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)#Articolul 148|Art. 148]]** — Regulated connection to upstream pipelines, transmission, LNG, distribution. Eligible applicants for upstream/TSO connection include distribution concessionaires, large industrial customers (>100,000 MWh/year), licensed distributors, gas producers, **biomethane producers**, etc. Non-household applicants must keep destination for 5 years or repay connection costs (art. 148(4)–(5)). Refusal grounds → arts. 149–150; financing of extensions → art. 151 (biomethane producers bear injection/connection costs under art. 151(1³)).

## Open questions

- How far have ANRE secondary acts (connection regulation, market rules, FUI designation regulation, switching platform) been re-issued under the OUG 143/2021 article bases — each HANRE needs a separate currency check.
- Interaction of art. 80 retail-price intervention rules with the temporary crisis/compensation OUGs (2021–2024 vintage) still affecting invoices.
- Storage licensing thresholds and co-location with generation under art. 10 — edge cases for hybrid parks.
- Biomethane quality ordinance under OUG 9/2026 art. III — deadline and content once ANRE adopts.
- Whether gas Title will be rewritten for the 2024 gas & hydrogen package or continue the amend-in-place pattern.
- Closed distribution systems (art. 50) and citizen-energy-community networks — how they interact with concession DSOs in practice.
