# OUG 134/2024 — storage in L123

## What it does

Amends **L123/2012** to embed **electricity storage** in primary law: definitions (standalone storage, co-located storage, storage operator), licensing hooks (art. 10), and **tariff policy** mandating ANRE to remove double taxation of stored energy. Transposes elements of **Dir. 2019/944** / **Reg. 2019/943** storage provisions.
