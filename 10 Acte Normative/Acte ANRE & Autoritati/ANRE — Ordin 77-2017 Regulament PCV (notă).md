# Memoriu Juridic — Ordinul ANRE nr. 77/2017 — Regulamentul Pieței de Certificate Verzi (PCV)

## Executive briefing

| Theme | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **GC market framework** | Organises trading, registration, and settlement of **certificate verzi** under L220/2008 |
| **OPCOM as PCV operator** | OPCOM S.A. runs the organised green-certificate market per ANRE designation |
| **Mandatory quota system** | Suppliers must acquire GCs to cover renewable quota obligations — links to L220 support scheme |
| **CfD interaction** | New RES projects increasingly under CfD (HG 318/2024) rather than GC-only revenue — dual-track analysis required |
| **Amendment velocity** | Eight amending ordinances 2017–2025 — verify current consolidare before advising |

## What it is / scope

Approves the **Regulament de organizare și funcționare a Pieței de Certificate Verzi**, setting:

- organisation and operation of the GC market;
- parties and responsibilities (producers, suppliers, OPCOM, ANRE);
- registration and information management for GC transactions;
- trading sessions, pricing, and transfer rules;
- sanctions and dispute resolution.

## Institutional architecture

```
ANRE (regulator / GC issuance rules)
        │
        ▼
OPCOM S.A. (PCV market operator — trading platform)
        │
        ├── Producers (GC recipients under L220/2008)
        └── Suppliers (mandatory GC acquisition for quota compliance)
```

## Key operational areas

### Registration & account management

- Participants must register with the PCV system before trading.
- GC accounts track issuance, transfer, surrender, and cancellation.

### Trading & pricing

- Organised trading sessions on OPCOM platform.
- Price formation subject to ANRE market-monitoring powers under [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]].

### Compliance & quota

- Electricity suppliers demonstrate RES quota compliance by surrendering GCs.
- Shortfall triggers penalties under L220/2008 and ANRE enforcement.

## CfD vs. GC — strategic context

| Mechanism | Legal base | Revenue model |
|---|---|---|
| **Certificate verzi** | L220/2008 + Ord. 77/2017 | Market-priced GCs + mandatory supplier quota |
| **Contracts for Difference** | HG 318/2024 + Ord. 52/2024 | 15-year strike-price difference payments |

New onshore wind/solar increasingly competes via **CfD auctions**; legacy and smaller projects may still rely on GC revenue. M&A due diligence must identify which support regime applies per asset.
