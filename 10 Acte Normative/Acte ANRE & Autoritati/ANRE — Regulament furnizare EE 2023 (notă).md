# Regulament furnizare — practitioner map

## Scope

Governs **retail supply** to final customers: contracts, metering, billing, switching, universal service / last-resort transfer mechanics, and supplier obligations. Operationalises liberalised supply under L123 + OUG 143/2021 consumer-rights layer.

## DD priorities

1. **Supply contract** — price type, indexation, termination, collateral.
2. **Switching** — notice periods and default supplier transfer triggers.
3. **FUI transfer** — when portfolio passes to last-resort supplier — [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă instanță]].
4. **Metering & billing** — data access for producers with behind-the-meter load.
