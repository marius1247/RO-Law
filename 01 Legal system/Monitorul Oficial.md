# Monitorul Oficial al României

The **official gazette**. Normative acts acquire legal effect through publication here (subject to the entry-into-force rule the act itself states). Every act in `10 Legislation` should carry its MO reference in frontmatter (`mo_publicare`).

## Structure (practical)
| Part | Typical content |
|---|---|
| **Partea I** | Laws, OUGs, ordinances, HGs, and other normative acts of general application |
| **Partea a II-a** | International acts, treaties (as published) |
| **Partea a III-a / IV-a** | Individual / collective acts, announcements (less relevant to this vault’s normative corpus) |
| **Partea a VI-a** | Public procurement notices (bridge to procurement practice) |

ANRE *Ordine* with normative character are published in **Partea I** — that is the publicity condition for the regulatory cascade.

## Why it matters
- **Publicity condition:** as a rule, a normative act enters into force **three days after publication** in the MO, or on the later date it specifies (Const. art. 78 for laws; parallel rules for other acts). Unpublished normative acts do not produce effects *erga omnes*.
- **Citation:** canonical form includes MO number and date — e.g. Constitution republication *MO nr. 767/31.10.2003*.
- **Versioning:** amendments are themselves published in the MO; [Portal Legislativ](https://legislatie.just.ro) then produces **consolidated** versions. This is why version-tracking matters — see [[Currency ledger]] · [[Conventions]].
