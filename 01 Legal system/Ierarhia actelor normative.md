# Ierarhia actelor normative (hierarchy of normative acts)

The backbone of this vault. Romania is a **civil-law** system: written normative acts, ranked by legal force, are the primary source of law. The categories are set by the **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)|Constitution]]** (esp. arts. 73, 108, 115) and by drafting rules in **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)|Legea 24/2000]]**.

## Executive briefing

- **Strict formal hierarchy:** lower-level acts cannot alter, suspend, or contradict higher-level acts.
- **Conflict resolution:** *lex superior derogat legi inferiori* · *lex specialis derogat legi generali* · *lex posterior derogat legi priori*.
- **Executive overreach risk:** HG and agency orders that exceed primary-law delegation are vulnerable to administrative annulment or EU-law disapplication.

## Constitutional anchors
| Article | What it fixes |
|---|---|
| **Art. 73** | Categories of laws: **organic** vs **ordinary**; organic-law domains and adoption majority |
| **Art. 108** | **Hotărâri** and **ordonanțe** of the Government |
| **Art. 115** | **Ordonanțe de urgență** — conditions, limits, parliamentary approval, and the emergency peculiarity of the RO system |

→ [[Concept — Ordonanță de urgență]] · [[Procesul legislativ]]

## Working ranking — `forta_juridica` 1–8
Matches [[Conventions]] frontmatter. Highest → lowest:

| # | Category | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Constituție** | Supreme law; all acts must conform |
| **2** | **Legi de revizuire a Constituției** | Titlul VII procedure; special majorities / referendum |
| **3** | **Legi organice & legi ordinare** (incl. codes) | Organic reserved matters — Const. art. 73; codes are laws by form |
| **4** | **Ordonanțe de urgență** | Const. art. 115; force of law pending approval; can amend statutes |
| **5** | **Ordonanțe** (simple / enabling) | Const. art. 108 / 115 enabling-law track |
| **6** | **Hotărâri ale Guvernului (HG)** | Const. art. 108 — organisation & execution of laws |
| **7** | **Acte ale ministerelor / autorităților centrale de specialitate** | Orders, instructions within competence |
| **8** | **Acte ale autorităților autonome & APL** | e.g. [[ANRE]] *Ordine*, local council decisions |

> [!warning] Organic vs ordinary
> Whether a matter *must* be an organic law is fixed by Const. art. 73. Getting this wrong is a common source of constitutional challenges. OUGs that touch organic-law domains face stricter constitutional scrutiny.

> [!important] OUG vs lege in practice
> An approved OUG sits with laws for operational reading; an unapproved or rejected OUG is a live constitutional and transitional-law problem. Always check parliamentary status before relying on a recent OUG that rewrites market design or tax.

## EU law
As an EU Member State, Romania applies **EU primary and secondary law** with primacy over contrary domestic law (Const. art. 148). That is a *horizontal* overlay, not a rung that replaces the domestic scale above — but for energy and competition it often decides the outcome. → `EU Law/` vault.

## International treaties
Ratified treaties bind the state; human-rights treaties and EU law have special constitutional status. Publication and entry into force still run through [[Monitorul Oficial]] for domestic implementing acts.

## Conflict resolution matrix

| Conflict type | Governing principle | Operational effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Hierarchical** (e.g. ANRE order vs. L123/2012) | *Lex superior derogat legi inferiori* | Lower act illegal — annulment / disapplication |
| **Temporal** (2024 law vs. 2020 law) | *Lex posterior derogat legi priori* | Newer norm prevails |
| **Generality** (Civil Code vs. L31/1990) | *Lex specialis derogat legi generali* | Special law applies; general law as fallback |
| **National vs. EU** | Const. art. 148 / EU primacy | Courts disapply conflicting national law |

## Strategic & regulatory risk notes

1. **Challenging administrative excess:** When an agency order imposes penalties or obligations not grounded in primary law, file under **Legea 554/2004** requesting suspension (*suspendarea executării*) and annulment (*anularea actului*).
2. **Contract drafting:** In long-term energy or infrastructure contracts, do not rely solely on secondary orders — incorporate fallback clauses referencing primary statutes and EU thresholds.
