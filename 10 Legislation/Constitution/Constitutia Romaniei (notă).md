# Constituția României — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Constitutia Romaniei (text)]] · **Legislative technique:** [[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)|L24/2000]] · **Hierarchy:** [[Ierarhia actelor normative]]

## Executive briefing

| Theme | Operational takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Architectural supremacy** | All normative acts must conform; CCR review (*a priori* / *a posteriori*) can invalidate statutes mid-litigation |
| **EU law primacy (art. 148)** | Binding EU treaties and regulations prevail over conflicting domestic law; ordinary courts disapply national rules without prior CCR authorisation |
| **OUG risk** | Emergency ordinances enter force fast, bypass full parliamentary debate, and are frequently challenged — monitor MO + parliamentary approval track |
| **Economic rights** | Arts. 41, 44, 45 anchor labour protection, property guarantees, and free enterprise — baseline for expropriation, licensing, and regulatory takings analysis |
| **CCR temporal effects (art. 147)** | Unconstitutionality decisions apply *ex nunc* but benefit parties in active litigation; 45-day suspension window for Parliament to align |

## What it is / scope

Romania's supreme law (republication after the 2003 revision). For this vault it is the **hierarchy and procedure map**: categories of laws, Government acts (HG / OG / **OUG**), Constitutional Court review, and EU law primacy after accession. Almost every primary energy or tax amendment in recent years has arrived as an OUG — so arts. 108 and 115 are operational reading, not theory.

## Structure at a glance

- Title I — General principles (state, sovereignty, territory…)
- Title II — Fundamental rights, freedoms, duties
- Title III — Public authorities (Parliament, President, Government, administration, judiciary)
- Title IV — Economy & public finance
- Title V — Constitutional Court
- Title VI — Euro-Atlantic integration (incl. EU)
- Title VII — Revision; Title VIII — Final / transitional

## Key provisions

- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Romanian state: national, sovereign, independent, unitary, indivisible; republican form; rule-of-law / democratic / social state; separation of powers; supremacy of the Constitution and of the laws.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 15|Art. 15]]** — Universality of rights and duties; **non-retroactivity** of civil law (except more favourable criminal / contravention law) — the baseline when OUGs rewrite tax or energy rights mid-stream.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 61|Art. 61]]** — Parliament as supreme representative body and sole legislative authority; bicameral (Camera Deputaților + Senat).
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 73|Art. 73]]** — Categories of laws: constitutional, **organic**, ordinary. Organic-law domains listed (elections, parties, government organisation, emergency/siege, criminal offences, civil service, administrative litigation, courts, property, education, local autonomy, labour, national minorities, cults, plus other Const.-mandated organic fields). Energy market statutes are typically ordinary laws; ANRE as autonomous authority ties to organic-law logic via art. 117(3).
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 108|Art. 108]]** — Government acts: **hotărâri** (organise execution of laws) and **ordonanțe** (under an enabling law). Publication in MO is constitutive; non-publication ⇒ inexistence.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 115|Art. 115]]** — **Legislative delegation.** Ordinary ordinances under enabling law (not in organic domains). **OUGs** only in extraordinary situations that cannot be postponed, with reasons; enter into force after filing with the competent Chamber **and** MO publication; organic-norm OUGs need organic majority; hard limits (no constitutional-law domain; cannot affect fundamental institutions, constitutional rights/duties, electoral rights, or forced transfer to public property). Approval/rejection by law; transitional effects regulated in that law. *This is the constitutional bottleneck for OUG 33/2007, OUG 143/2021, tax OUGs, etc.*
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 146|Art. 146]]** — CCR attributions: a priori / a posteriori constitutionality, treaties, Parliament regulations, legal disputes of a constitutional nature, parties, referendum, presidential contests, etc.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 148|Art. 148]]** — EU integration: transfer of powers; obligation to transpose; **priority of EU founding treaties and binding acts** over contrary domestic provisions, subject to the Act of Accession.

### Economic & property rights (commercial relevance)

- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 41|Art. 41]]** — Right to work; protection of working conditions; minimum wage; collective bargaining.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 44|Art. 44]]** — Private property guaranteed equally; expropriation only for *public utility* (*utilitate publică*), by law, with **just and prior compensation**.
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 45|Art. 45]]** — Free access to economic activity and free enterprise under conditions set by law.

### Executive legislation — OUG risk matrix

| Mechanism | Trigger | Review | Business risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OG** (simple ordinance) | Enabling law from Parliament | Standard post-publication review | Moderate — predictable during recess |
| **OUG** (emergency ordinance) | Extraordinary circumstances; regulation cannot be delayed | CCR via exception; Avocatul Poporului may challenge directly | **High** — immediate force, retroactive instability, later invalidation risk |

### CCR — exception of unconstitutionality

- Raised before ordinary trial or appellate courts during ongoing litigation.
- If accepted, CCR may declare the challenged provision unconstitutional.
- Under **art. 147**: unconstitutional provisions are suspended 45 days; if not aligned, legal effects cease permanently.

## Strategic & compliance notes

1. **Litigation:** In high-stakes commercial or regulatory disputes involving state entities, evaluate whether the statutory basis can be challenged via an *excepție de neconstituționalitate* or an EU-law conflict plea under art. 148.
2. **Regulatory monitoring:** Track OUG drafts during public consultation — tax, energy, and environmental regimes often change abruptly via OUG.
