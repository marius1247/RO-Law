# Regulament racordare EE (Ord. 59/2013) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[ANRE — Regulament racordare EE 2013 (text)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] · [[Concept — Licență în energetică]] · **Parent:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012 art. 25]] · **DD index:** [[_Energy Investment Due Diligence Index]]

> [!note] Portal ID
> Full regulament text is portal **160289** (not the Ord. shell dump `150711`). Heavily amended since 2013 — always verify consolidare before quoting deadlines or guarantee %.

## Thesis

Procedural code for attaching users (consumers, producers, storage) to public electricity networks: **application → solution study / solution sheet → ATR (aviz tehnic de racordare) → connection contract → works → connection certificate → energisation**. For RES / BESS project finance, the ATR + reinforcement-cost + financial-guarantee regime is usually the critical-path legal instrument.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Trigger events (art. 5)
User must apply for connection (new consumption/production site; separation for direct connection) or for **ATR / certificate update** when: power increase; technical/admin change without power increase; connection-installation change; behind-the-meter generators exporting upstream. Operator updates ATR/certificate *ex officio* and free when its own network changes force a solution change (art. 5(5)–(6)) — updated connection tariff cannot exceed the original offer.

### Who is the competent operator
- Default: network operator to whose network the user connects.
- **>50 MW** production installed or simultaneous consumption → application to **TSO** (art. 8).
- OD treated as user vis-à-vis upstream operator (art. 2(2)).

### Solution study clocks (art. 18)
- Study paid by applicant (deviz; surplus refunded).
- Delivery: **≤3 months** (≥110 kV) / **≤1 month** (MV/LV) from study contract; extendable to 6 / 2 months if justified in the proposal.
- If multiple solutions, user must choose in writing within **2 months** or the study lapses and the application is closed.

### ATR content — the bankability checklist (arts. 24–28)
ATR is the operator’s **offer** to the connection request. Must state, as applicable: approved import/export power and phasing; installed vs approved export justification; solution description (connection point, delimitation point, metering point, **upstream reinforcement works**); user-specific conditions & commissioning tests; SCADA / control; connection tariff; **guarantee value/form (art. 31)**; continuity level; protection/automation; ATR validity; etc.

### Financial guarantee (art. 31) — >1 MW + reinforcement
When new site or power increase yields approved power **>1 MW** and upstream reinforcement is needed:
- Operator requires a financial guarantee (SBLC, avalised cheque, term deposit, or other accepted form) as % of connection tariff (cap historically 20% — art. 69; **verify current %** in consolidare).
- **Production sites:** constitute guarantee **before** connection contract, within **3 months** of ATR — else ATR dies (art. 33(1)(a)).
- **Consumption sites:** constitute per connection-contract deadline.
- Call events: missed connection-tariff payments; failure to build/user-installation dossier; missed development milestones; (production) failure to request connection contract within ATR validity.
- Released on ATR expiry with certificate for final power, contract rescission (except call events), or user pre-funding of operator’s reinforcement share under art. 43(3)(d).
- Called amounts → operator revenue earmarked for reinforcement in the ANRE-approved investment plan.

### ATR validity / death (arts. 32–33)
ATR lives until connection certificate for final approved power, unless earlier:
- **3 months** (production / hybrid with export >1 MW) without proof of guarantee;
- **12 months** without connection contract;
- connection-contract rescission;
- building-permit / underlying approval expiry or final court annulment.

### Reinforcement cost allocation (arts. 41–43)
- Upstream reinforcement for export power: operator builds; user’s share inside connection tariff per ANRE connection-tariff methodology.
- If operator cannot deliver reinforcement by user’s energisation date, ATR must disclose delay reasons, possible dates, and max power available **without** reinforcement. User may: abandon; wait; stage development within available power; or **pre-fund** the operator’s share with later recovery (art. 43(3)).

### Works & energisation clocks (selected)
- LV branch (branșament): design + building permit + execution + commissioning reception ≤ **90 calendar days** from connection contract if tariff paid.
- User may designate an attested designer/contractor for connection installation; operator recalculates tariff in 3 working days (art. 44 area).
- Energisation after connection certificate + supply/transport/distribution contract — typically ≤ **10 calendar days** (households and standard case).

### Behind-the-meter generation (art. 7)
Three paths: (a) export surplus via existing consumption connection (update certificate for export power); (b) no-export (may only need utilisation dossier update); (c) separate production site with new ATR.

## Obligations

| Who | Duty |
|---|---|
| Network operators | Non-discriminatory connection; study/ATR clocks; publish procedures (ANRE-endorsed for guarantee/reinforcement rules); monthly TSO reporting on large ATRs |
| Users | Complete file; pay study/ATR/connection tariff; constitute guarantee; build utilisation installation; meet milestones |
| >50 MW applicants | File with TSO |

## Enforcement

Operator non-compliance with clocks / refusal → ANRE complaint (L123 art. 25 dispute path — 2 months) → court. Guarantee calls and ATR expiry are the private-law pressure points for inactive projects (queue hygiene).

## Hard edges

- **2013 baseline, many amendments** — queue guarantees, readiness milestones and market-based capacity allocation under L123 art. 25(2²) may sit in later Ordine; never rely on the 2013 bare text alone for live projects.
- Storage: apply art. 25(14) L123 (OUG 134) when reading solution-study duties.
- Portal `150711` is the wrong dump (Ord. shell) — working text is `160289`.
- Connection-tariff methodology is a **separate** ANRE act; this regulament states *when* tariff/guarantee apply, not the lei formula.
