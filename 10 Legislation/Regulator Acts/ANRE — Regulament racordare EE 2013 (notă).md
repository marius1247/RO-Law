# Regulament racordare — practitioner map

## Scope

Sets the **connection procedure** for users attaching to public electricity networks: application, **Aviz tehnic de racordare (ATR)**, connection agreement, works, energisation, cost allocation, and maximum time limits. Applies to consumers, producers, and storage installations.

## DD priorities

1. **ATR** — capacity (MW/MVA), voltage level, expiry, conditions, reinforcement obligations.
2. **Cost allocation** — shallow vs deep charging; who pays grid reinforcement.
3. **Queue / readiness** — financial guarantees and milestone dates (later amendments — verify consolidare).
4. **Appeal** — operator decision → [[ANRE]] → court.
