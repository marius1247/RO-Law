# Ordin ANRE 51/2024 — CfD contribution methodology — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[ANRE — Metodologie contributie CfD 2024 (text)]] · **Collection contracts:** [[ANRE — Ordin 52-2024 Contracte-cadru CfD (notă)]] · **Parent HG:** [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Contributie CfD]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

## Thesis

This methodology sets **how the lei/kWh CfD contribution is calculated and collected** to fund administration of Romania’s low-carbon CfD scheme: costs of **Scheme Operator (Transelectrica)** and **CfD Counterparty (OPCOM)**, plus scheme corrections, recovered from essentially **all electricity consumed in Romania** via suppliers / self-supplying producers. It is the **rate-building** sister to Ord. 52/2024’s **contractual billing engine**.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Problem A — What is being recovered?
Art. 1: monthly contribution into the **CfD liquidity fund** covering Scheme Operator + Counterparty administration. Art. 5 splits unit rate into **C_op.sch** + **C_contraparte**. Annual pot includes estimated admin costs, **profit ≤ 5%**, and corrections from the regularisation period (with EU reference interest — art. 7).

### Problem B — Who pays?
Arts. 1(3), 3, 4(2)(d):
- retail suppliers (incl. self-supply);
- producers for own final consumption that attracts distribution/transport tariff;
- producers supplying consumers on plant busbars where D/T tariff is due;
- (Ord. 52/2024 later expressly extends storage operators feeding own load — apply that contract layer on top).

End-consumers pay via the supply invoice (arts. 22–24); payers must show CfD as a **separate invoice line** (art. 23(b)).

### Problem C — Annual set (t+1)
Art. 5: filings by **1 Nov**; ANRE decides by **20 Dec**; in force **1 Jan t+1**. Unit rate = annual CfD amount / estimated kWh sold to RO consumers (incl. self-consumption forecasts, annex 2a).

### Problem D — Mid-year revision (H2)
Arts. 8–11: filings by **20 May**; if revised rate differs by **>2%** from in-force rate, ANRE Ordin by **20 Jun**, effective **1 Jul**.

### Problem E — Monthly cash cycle
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| by 10th | Payers report prior-month volumes to Counterparty (art. 14) |
| ≤15 days EOM | Scheme Operator sends verified data to Counterparty (art. 15(1)) |
| ≤20 days EOM | Counterparty invoices payers (art. 15(4)) |
| ≤7 days of receipt / by EOM following consumption | Payers pay (art. 16) |
| by 25th | Counterparty pays Scheme Operator share (art. 18(e)) |

Cross-check discrepancies → clarifications → escalate to ANRE (art. 15(2)). Guarantees via Counterparty procedure approved by ANRE (art. 17) — executed into liquidity fund (art. 19(f)).

## Obligations / enforcement

- Supply contracts **automatically supplemented** (art. 28) — no renegotiation needed to pass through.
- Late payment → contractual penalties + guarantee execution + ANRE notification (arts. 19, 23).
- Contestation path: challenge the **rate Ordin** / collection acts; methodology is the calculation law.

## Hard edges

- Contribution funds **admin + scheme corrections**, not the CfD strike differential itself as a separate consumer product — but corrections can embed scheme cash needs.
- **2% H2 trigger** means small drifts stay unrevised until next annual cycle.
- Storage-as-payer clarity lives primarily in **Ord. 52/2024** (Dec 2025 amendments) — always read both.
- Distinct litigation tracks: contribution contracts → ordinary courts (Ord. 52 note); CfD beneficiary contracts → CACI arbitration under HG 318.
