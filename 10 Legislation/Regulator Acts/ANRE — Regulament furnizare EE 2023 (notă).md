# Regulament furnizare EE (Ord. 5/2023) — Analysis

## Thesis

Retail-supply operating code for final customers: **contracts, POSF switching, billing, universal service vs last-resort (FUI), vulnerable customers, and supplier obligations**. It is the day-to-day instrument that makes OUG 143/2021 consumer rights executable — and the DD checklist for any supply M&A, offtaker diligence, or behind-the-meter producer with retail load.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Three supply regimes (art. 4(8))
- **Competitive** — negotiated or offer-type prices.
- **Universal service** — ANRE framework contract + universal-service offers.
- **Last resort (FUI)** — only ANRE-designated suppliers; ANRE FUI framework contract. Concept → [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă instanță]].

Licence generally required (art. 4(6)); exceptions for direct supply from own generation installations and certain non-concession OD supplies (art. 4(7)).

### Contracting & POSF (arts. 12–14)
- Mandatory content catalogue (art. 12) — identity, price structure, duration, termination, complaints, etc.
- **Anti-lock-in** (art. 13): clauses that impede supplier switching or active-customer / aggregation participation are forbidden.
- Contracting via **POSF** or equivalent channels that still respect the POSF regulation (art. 14); supplier websites must link to POSF; max **5 working days** to conclude once complete file received (POSF has its own clock).

### Dynamic prices (art. 16)
Suppliers with **>200,000** customers must offer dynamic-price contracts to customers with smart meters (SMI), on request — OUG 143 art. 3 pct. 32 made operational.

### Switching & termination (arts. 18, 20)
- Customer may switch supplier / aggregator under POSF rules (art. 20).
- Early termination fees on fixed-term fixed-price contracts allowed only if pre-disclosed, contractual, and limited to direct economic loss (art. 18(6)–(8)); ANRE must be informed of fee methodology.
- Supplier rescission for non-payment: disconnection pre-notice → ≥15-day rescission notice (art. 18(5)).
- Licence expiry/withdrawal ends the contract (art. 18(1)(e)) — portfolio then follows FUI rules.

### Billing stack (art. 22 area)
Invoice must break out: energy price, **TD + TL extraction + SS**, CV, cogeneration contribution, taxes, and (post-Apr 2023) extensive information annex (comparison tool link, switching rights, fuel mix, etc.). Network tariff changes apply from their entry-into-force date with invoice notification deemed sufficient.

### Own-consumption / producer sites (art. 4(2)–(4))
Suppliers, network operators and producers supplying own sites must still meet retail payment obligations, balancing responsibility, and transport/distribution contracts — relevant for hybrid generator–consumer structures.

## Obligations

| Who | Highlights |
|---|---|
| Suppliers | Publish offer-types (incl. in ANRE comparator); POSF link; 5-day contracting; billing detail; vulnerable-customer facilities; no switching barriers |
| Large suppliers (>200k) | Dynamic-price product for SMI customers |
| Customers | Pay invoices; may switch / unilaterally denounce under contract |
| OD | Metering / disconnection interface; notified on contract end (except FUI takeover) |

## Enforcement

Performance standard for supply + ANRE complaint / sanction route under L123 / OUG 33. POSF non-compliance is both regulatory and operational (switch fails). Early-exit fee methodology is ANRE-notifiable.

## Hard edges

- FUI designation / takeover mechanics live in a **separate** FUI regulament — this act points to it but does not contain the full designation procedure.
- Crisis / compensation OUGs (2021–2024 vintage) can override invoice price lines — always check temporal legislation against art. 22.
