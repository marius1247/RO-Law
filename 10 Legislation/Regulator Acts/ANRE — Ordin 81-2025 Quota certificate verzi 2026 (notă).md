# Ordin ANRE 81/2025 — Estimated GC quota for 2026 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[ANRE — Ordin 81-2025 Quota certificate verzi 2026 (text)]] · **Methodology:** [[ANRE — Metodologie quota certificate verzi 2022 (notă)|Ord. 96/2022]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Cota obligatorie certificate verzi]] · [[Concept — Certificat verde]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]]

## Thesis

Annual **estimated-quota Ordin** under L220 arts. 4(7), 8(8) and Ord. 96/2022 arts. 5–8: sets **`c_cv,estimată,2026 = 0,499387 CV/MWh`**, applicable from **1 January 2026**. This is the number used for **2026 quarterly compliance and fines**; the final realised 2026 quota will only appear by **1 March 2027**.

## Architecture

Four articles + annex of calculation inputs. Pure rate instrument — no new obligations beyond those already in L220 / Ord. 96/2022.

## Operative regime

### What was decided
Art. 1 + Annex: estimated mandatory purchase quota **0,499387 CV per MWh** of obligated final consumption for 2026.

### Annex inputs (Ord. 96/2022 math made explicit)

| # | Input | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Estimated GC bill impact (L220 art. 4(7)) | **14,5 €/MWh** |
| 2 | BNR average FX — 11 months 2025 | **5,0370 lei/€** |
| 3 | Estimated final consumption 2026 | **48.720.645 MWh** |
| 4 | Estimated exempted consumption 2026 | **6.637.592 MWh** |
| 5 | PCSCV weighted avg price — 11 months 2025 | **146,2532 lei/CV** |
| 6 | Quota | `(14,5 × 5,0370) / 146,2532` = **0,499387** |

Note: row 6 uses the ICV×FX / PCSCV-price identity from arts. 5–6; consumption rows document the exemption-adjusted base but cancel in this simplified published identity.

### Who must act
Art. 2: OPCOM (as OPCV), RES producers, and all obligated operators under art. 1. From 1 Jan 2026, quarterly need = obligated MWh × **0,499387** (rounded per art. 10 Ord. 96/2022).

## Hard edges

- This is the **estimated** quota — do not treat as final annual close.
- PCSCV price in the denominator means a **higher GC spot price → lower quota** for the same ICV ceiling — liquidity and price path feed the obligation size.
- Exemption volume (~6,6 TWh) is material to the system but not to the published quota identity once ICV/price are fixed.
