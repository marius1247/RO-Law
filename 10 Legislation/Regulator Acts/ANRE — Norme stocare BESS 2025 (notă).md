# Ord. 56/2025 — BESS tariff exemption norme

## What it does

Implements **OUG 134/2024** policy: for energy **withdrawn from the grid, stored, and re-injected**, exempt **TL, SS, TD, and CV obligation** on the stored quantity at re-injection. Charging leg and auxiliary/loss consumption remain fully tariffed.

## Practitioner hooks

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Metering** | Operator determines stored quantity; reporting to ANRE |
| **Interim regime** | Art. 5 — valid until permanent cost–benefit storage tariff methodology |
| **Scope limit** | Co-located RES → storage → grid may not inherit exemptions automatically |
