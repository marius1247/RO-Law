## How methodologies relate to snapshot tariffs

1. ANRE approves **methodology** (durable rules).
2. Operator submits revenue requirement under methodology.
3. ANRE issues **Ordin** fixing unit tariffs (e.g. [[ANRE — Ordin 74-2025 Tarife transport EE (text)|Ord. 74/2025]]).
4. Individual **Decizii** per operator — **out of vault scope**; pull live when advising.

## Practitioner use

- Explain **why** TG/SS/TD changed — read methodology revenue-building blocks.
- Model **SS volatility** — Ord. 116/2022 + frequent SS Ordine (60/2025 → 73/2025).
- **GC quota** trajectory — methodology + annual Ordin (e.g. [[ANRE — Ordin 81-2025 Quota certificate verzi 2026 (text)|Ord. 81/2025]]).
