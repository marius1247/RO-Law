## How methodologies relate to snapshot tariffs

1. ANRE approves **methodology** (durable rules).
2. Operator submits revenue / cost forecast under methodology.
3. ANRE issues **Ordin** fixing unit tariffs — e.g. [[ANRE — Ordin 74-2025 Tarife transport EE (notă)|Ord. 74/2025]] (TG/TL), Ord. 73/2025 (SS), Ord. 76/77/2025 (TD).
4. Individual **Decizii** per operator — **out of vault scope**; pull live when advising.
