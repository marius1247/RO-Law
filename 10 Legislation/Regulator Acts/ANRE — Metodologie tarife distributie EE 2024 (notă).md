# Ordin ANRE 67/2024 — Distribution tariff methodology — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[ANRE — Metodologie tarife distributie EE 2024 (text)]] · **2026 rates:** [[ANRE — Ordin 76-2025 Tarife distributie DEER (text)|Ord. 76/2025 Delgaz]] · [[ANRE — Ordin 77-2025 Tarife distributie Delgaz (text)|Ord. 77/2025 DEER]] *(filenames swap operator labels — verify annex)* · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Concept:** [[Concept — Tarif de distributie TD]] · **Index:** [[ANRE — Metodologii tarifare EE (notă)]]

## Thesis

Sister instrument to the transport methodology: builds each **concessionaire DSO’s** regulated distribution revenue and voltage-specific **TD** unit tariffs under a five-year **tariff-basket cap** (*coș de tarife plafon*), not a single revenue-cap scalar. Practitioners use it to explain **why** MT/IT/JT components move, how **CPT** and capitalised CPT sit inside TD, and what costs a DSO cannot put into the basket.

| Later arts | Corrections, inflation, quality, **specific tariffs by voltage**, billing rules |

**Voltage bands (art. 5):** IT = 110 kV · MT = 1–110 kV excl. · JT ≤ 1 kV (0.4 kV standard).

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Problem A — Cap design differs from transport
- Transport = **venit plafon** (Ord. 68/2024).
- Distribution = **coș de tarife plafon**: upper bound on the **quantity-weighted average** of the OD’s tariff structure (art. 1(2), art. 5 definition).
- Same incentive DNA: efficiency sharing, RAB/RRR, CPT targets, annual approval (art. 9).

### Problem B — Who pays which TD?
- TD applies under distribution contracts to users connected to that OD’s RED, **by voltage of injection/extraction** at the delimitation point (art. 4).
- Producers on MT typically face the **MT** specific tariff (and stacked components — see concept).
- Service includes active energy distribution, reactive, and metering within licence conditions (art. 6(2)).

### Problem C — What else rides in the cost base?
Art. 6(3) pulls in revenues/costs of activities that use BAR assets or distribution staff: ATR / connection works / solution studies / pole rentals (regulated), plus listed non-regulated activities (transformer rentals, waste, asset sales, IT/telecom services) — allocation rules prevent cross-subsidy (arts. 17, 21).

### Problem D — Mergers of DSOs
Arts. 10–11: after merger, **legacy zonal tariffs** can persist until period end; OD must keep separate cost/revenue books by pre-merger zones; merger efficiency benefits are shared with users under art. 105.

### Problem E — Cost hygiene (same family as transport)
Arts. 19–22: necessary / opportune / efficient / market-conditions test; common-cost allocation by revenue keys unless ANRE approves change; single-counting rule (art. 8).

## Obligations

| Who | Duty |
|---|---|
| **OD** | Submit forecasts in real terms of reference year; management accounts by regulated / non-regulated activity |
| **OD** | Minimum own-funded investment vs amortization (definitions art. 5) |
| **Shareholders / OD** | Pre-merger benefit undertaking ≥ 30 days before merger (art. 11) |
| **ANRE** | Period revenues + annual TD per OD; corrections (arts. 15–16) |

## Enforcement

- Refusal of unjustified costs (art. 19(3)).
- Correction reopeners parallel to transport (art. 15: bad data, errors, double count, legislative shock, power-price shock affecting CPT).
- Contencios against approving Ordin / Decizie — methodology itself is the calculation law.

## Hard edges

- Applies to **concessionaire** ODs — not every licence holder without concession.
- **Unique tariff per OD network**, with merger exception (art. 10).
- Connection works funded by users are not free RAB gifts — contribution / reimbursement mechanics interact with BAR (see methodology investment chapters + Ord. 98/2022 investment procedure).
- SS and TG/TL are **other** instruments; a MT producer stack = TD (DSO) + TG + SS (+ TL if withdrawing).
