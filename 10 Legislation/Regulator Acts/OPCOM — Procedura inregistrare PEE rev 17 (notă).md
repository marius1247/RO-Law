# Memoriu Tehnico-Juridic — Procedura OPCOM privind Înregistrarea Participanților la Piețele Organizate de EE (Rev. 17)

## Executive briefing

| Theme | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Market-access gate** | Mandatory procedure before trading on OPCOM-organised electricity markets (PZU, IDM, forward, etc.) |
| **Participation Convention** | Registration culminates in the **Convenția de Participare** and associated annexes (guarantees, operational contacts) |
| **Licensing prerequisite** | Applicants must hold valid ANRE licences / authorisations where required by [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]] |

## What it is / scope

OPCOM S.A., as **Operator al Piețelor de Energie Electrică Desemnat (OPEED)**, sets the contractual and operational conditions for becoming a **Participant la Piețele Organizate de Energie Electrică (PEE)**. The procedure covers:

- eligibility and documentation;
- registration workflow and OPCOM approval;
- Convenția de Participare and standard annexes;
- account setup, IT access, and operational contacts;
- suspension and termination of participation.

## Registration workflow (summary)

```
Applicant (licensed trader/supplier/producer as applicable)
        │
        ▼ Submit registration file + annexes (ANRE licences, company docs, guarantees)
OPCOM review & approval
        │
        ▼ Execute Convenția de Participare + Act adițional model
Live market access (PZU / IDM / forward per product election)
        │
        ▼ Ongoing: guarantees, NF procedures, PO-PZU compliance
```

## Legal & commercial risks

1. **Gun-jumping analogue:** Trading before formal OPCOM registration is a market-rule violation — separate from competition gun-jumping but equally operationally critical.
2. **Guarantee shortfall:** OPCOM may suspend participation if financial collateral falls below thresholds — see [[OPCOM — Procedura garantii PZU-PI 2025 (notă)]].
3. **Licence mismatch:** Registration category must match ANRE licence scope (trading vs. supply vs. generation).

## Strategic notes

1. **New market entrants:** Budget 8–12 weeks for registration + guarantee setup + IT testing.
2. **M&A:** Verify target's Convenția de Participare status, guarantee levels, and any OPCOM suspension history.
