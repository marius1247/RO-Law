> [!warning] Partially repealed — read with later ordinances
> Multiple ANRE ordinances have **abrogated chapters** of the 2004 Cod comercial (caps. 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 in whole or part — see repeal notes in the ordin text). Day-ahead settlement, guarantees, and several market segments now live in dedicated OPCOM procedures and later ANRE ordinances (e.g. Ord. 82/2014, 31/2018, 127/2021). **Always cross-check** whether a cited chapter remains in force.

# Memoriu Juridic — Ordinul ANRE nr. 25/2004 — Codul Comercial al Pieței Angro de EE

## Executive briefing

| Theme | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Foundational wholesale code** | First consolidated commercial rules for Romania's electricity wholesale market (effective 1 Jan 2005) |
| **Annex not in MO** | The Cod comercial annex was never published in Monitorul Oficial — binding via art. 1 approval + ANRE website publication |
| **Market structure** | Defines bilateral contracts market, day-ahead centralised market, balancing, and participant obligations |
| **Incremental repeal** | 2014–2022 ordinances stripped caps. 5–14; surviving chapters govern access, bilateral trading, and residual commercial rules |
| **PPA / OTC relevance** | Bilateral contract chapter remains the primary domestic reference for off-exchange corporate PPAs |

## Identificare

- **Act:** Ordinul președintelui ANRE nr. 25 din 22 octombrie 2004
- **Publicare:** MO nr. 989 / 27.10.2004; **intrare în vigoare:** 1 ianuarie 2005
- **Temei:** Legea energiei electrice nr. 318/2003 (predecessor to L123/2012)
- **Obiect:** aprobă **Codul comercial al pieței angro de energie electrică** (anexă integrantă)

## Structura pieței angro (anexă — caps. supraviețuitoare)

```
Acces la piață
    ├── Piața contractelor bilaterale (OTC / negociat)
    ├── Piața centralizată pentru Ziua Următoare (PZU) → parțial abrogată; OPCOM procedures
    ├── Piața intrazilnică (IDM)
    └── Dezechilibre / balancing → parțial migrat
```

### Bilateral contracts & PPAs

- Corporate and producer **power purchase agreements** executed off-exchange derive commercial discipline from the bilateral-market chapter.
- Post-liberalisation amendments to [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]] (OUG 143/2021 package) reinforce direct negotiated PPAs — read Cod comercial bilateral rules together with L123 licensing and ANRE Ord. 12/2023 (OPCOM trading regulation).

## Strategic notes

1. **Due diligence on legacy PPAs:** Confirm which Cod comercial version governed signing date and whether disputed clauses sit in repealed chapters.
2. **New market entry:** Pair this code with current OPCOM procedures — registration ([[OPCOM — Procedura inregistrare PEE rev 17 (notă)]]) is the operational gate, not Ord. 25 alone.
