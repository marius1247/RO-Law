# Legea 101/2016 — remedii & căi de atac (achiziții) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 101-2016 — remedii achizitii publice (text)]] · **Substantive twin:** [[Legea 98-2016 — achizitii publice (notă)|L98/2016]] · **Hub:** [[_Regulatory Risk & Litigation Matrix]]

## Thesis

Remedies statute paired with L98: **CNSC** challenges, court path, standstill, and ***cauțiune*** rules. Challenge strategy (CNSC vs court) and deposit timing usually decide whether a tender fight is viable.

## Architecture

CNSC competence & procedure · challenge deadlines · *cauțiune* · standstill / contract suspension · court appeals (CA București lineage).

## Operative regime

1. Identify contested act (award docs / exclusion / award decision).
2. Choose CNSC vs court path; calendar the short challenge window (verify live consolidare — historically ~10 days).
3. Post *cauțiune* correctly or the challenge dies.
4. Parallel bid-rigging exposure → [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21]].
