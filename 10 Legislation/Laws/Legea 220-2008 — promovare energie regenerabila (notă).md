# Legea nr. 220/2008 — promovarea energiei din surse regenerabile — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)]] ✅ · **Market statute:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] · **Regulator:** [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]]

> [!info] Currency
> Portal consolidare id `98742` (amendamente through Nov. 2025). Title lines in the dump are often empty — cite by **article number**. Accreditation window for new plants under the CV scheme closed end-2016; the law still governs legacy portfolios, trading and supplier quotas through ~2031–2032.

## What it is / scope

Romania's **green-certificate (certificat verde, CV)** support statute: a mandatory-quota / tradable-certificate scheme for electricity from renewable sources. It defines eligible technologies, support duration, how many certificates each MWh earns, who must buy certificates (suppliers and certain self-consumers), trading venues (OPCOM), price collar, and penalties for quota shortfall. It also carries older RES targets (2020) and network priority/guarantee rules that interact with L123/2012.

**Not** a general RED III transposition vehicle for new capacity — post-2016 renewables policy runs largely through other instruments (CfD / auctions, PPA market under L123, OUG packages). L220 remains the operating law for **legacy accredited plant** and the CV market that still appears as a separate line on end-user invoices.

Moldova parallel: `Moldovan Law/.../Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile` uses a different design (fixed tariffs / auctions / central buyer concepts under the newer EE law), not a green-certificate quota.

## Structure at a glance

- **Cap. I** — Purpose, definitions, eligible sources & support periods (arts. 1–3)
- **Cap. II** — Promotion system, national targets, ANRE accreditation, annual CV quotas & consumer-bill impact cap (arts. 4–8)
- **Cap. III** — CV trading (arts. 10–11)
- **Cap. IV** — Quota compliance & penalties (art. 12)
- **Cap. V** — Regional RES potential / ministry tasks (art. 13)
- **Cap. VI** — Sale of RES electricity; guaranteed / priority dispatch (art. 14)
- Later chapters: guarantees of origin, biofuels, administrative procedures, sanctions (many articles amended or repealed by OUG 163/2022 and others)

## Key provisions

- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Purpose: pull RES into the energy balance, local development, emissions reduction, guarantees of origin / administrative rules / grid connection for RES electricity, sustainability criteria for biofuels/bioliquids. Art. 1(2) institutes the promotion system for RES electricity.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 2|Art. 2]]** — Definitions: **certificat verde** (tradable title, not a financial instrument); annual mandatory CV purchase quota; mandatory RES electricity share under the scheme; biomass / biogas / bioliquid; accreditation; overcompensation / IRR tests; anonymous centralised CV market (OPCOM); average CV impact on the final consumer (lei/MWh). Cross-defines “energie din surse regenerabile” by reference to L123/2012 art. 3.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 3|Art. 3]]** — Eligible sources (hydro ≤10 MW, wind, solar, geothermal, biomass, bioliquids, biogas, landfill / sewage gas) and **support durations** (15 / 10 / 7 / 3 years by category). Critical cut-off: accreditation for plants commissioned/refurbished by **end-2016** (art. 3(3)). Storage co-located with accredited plant may keep CVs only for measured RES output (art. 3(3¹)). Trading of issued/deferred CVs can continue after accreditation expiry until **31 March 2032** (art. 3(3³)). Exclusions: imported industrial/municipal waste, pumped storage, own technological consumption, certain agricultural-land PV, etc.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 4|Art. 4]]** — National EE-from-RES targets (legacy 2010/2015/2020 percentages) and the annual **CV purchase quota** mechanism. From 2018 onward ANRE sets the prior-year quota so average final-consumer impact stays within a euro/MWh cap (e.g. 14.5 €/MWh from 2022) — the political safety valve that caps demand for CVs. Arts. 4(9²)–(9⁴): from 2023 ANRE may reduce the estimated impact and notify Government; design aims to absorb CVs issued 2017–2031 including deferred certificates.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 5|Art. 5]]** — Broader 2020 RES share target (24% of gross final energy) and ≥10% RES in transport — largely historical after RED II/III and OUG 163/2022 repealed parts of the chapter.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 6|Art. 6]]** — Issuance by the TSO: technology-specific CV multipliers per MWh (hydro / wind / biomass / solar, with post-2018 wind reduction and temporary deferrals 2013–2017 / 2017–2020 for solar). Extra CV for high-efficiency RES cogeneration and energy crops. State-aid investment support triggers a reduced CV count (art. 6(7)(b)). Validity of issued CVs: **12 months** (art. 6(9)).
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 8|Art. 8]]** — Who must buy CVs: suppliers (for final consumption and exports under bilateral agreements), producers for own final consumption, direct-line supply, etc. Quarterly and annual purchase obligations; CV cost billed **separately** on the electricity invoice; regularisation by 1 September. Industrial exemption mechanism (state-aid notified) at art. 8(8).
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 10|Art. 10]]** — Trading on centralised CV markets and bilateral CV contracts via OPCOM; generally **one sale** producer → supplier; ≥50% of annual obligation must be bought on the anonymous spot market (with legacy bilateral exceptions).
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 12|Art. 12]]** — ANRE compliance assessment; shortfall payment to the Environmental Fund (**70 €/CV** from 2018, previously 110 €); quarterly undershoot penalties; use of penalties for small household RES investments.
- **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)#Articolul 14|Art. 14]]** — RES electricity sold on the power market at market price; TSO/DSO priority dispatch and **guaranteed access** for contracted RES volumes — the operational bridge to L123/2012 arts. 25 and 27(3).

## How it connects

- **Domestic:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] (market, licensing, guaranteed take-up); ANRE secondary regulations on accreditation, CV issuance, billing; OPCOM market rules; Environmental Fund (OUG 196/2005).
- **EU:** originally Dir. 2009/28/EC; now read against `EU Law/10 Legislation/Directives/Dir 2023-2413 — RED III` and Governance Reg. 2018/1999 / PNIESC targets. State-aid decisions (e.g. Commission decision C(2016) 8865) constrain CV numbers when investment aid stacks.
- **MD:** `Moldovan Law/.../Legea 10-2016 — surse regenerabile` + central-buyer concepts in L164/2025 — different mechanism, same policy problem (bankable RES support without blowing consumer bills).

## Open questions

- Remaining volume of deferred CVs still to be released through 2030 and whether the art. 4 impact cap will bind again if demand falls.
- Interaction of art. 14 priority/guaranteed access with L123 congestion and market-based connection allocation (art. 25(2²)).
- Whether any new capacity can still touch this scheme indirectly (storage accreditation amendments) vs. full migration to CfD/other schemes.
- Invoice presentation and ANRE CV billing procedure — confirm against latest ANRE order, not only the SOAP-era assumptions in older commentaries.
