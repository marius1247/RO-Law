# Legea 126/2018 — piețe de instrumente financiare (MiFID II) — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 126-2018 — piete instrumente financiare (text)]] · **Issuers twin:** [[Legea 24-2017 — emitenti instrumente financiare (notă)|L24/2017]] · **Cluster hub:** [[Legea 208-2015 — piete de capital (notă)]] · **Energy wholesale contrast:** [[Concept — Participant PEE OPCOM]]

## Thesis

RO transposition of **MiFID II** — authorisation and conduct of investment firms (**SSIF**), trading venues, and related investor-protection rules. Relevant when an energy group builds a trading affiliate that may need an investment-firm licence **in addition to** ANRE / OPCOM market access.

## Architecture

Investment-firm authorisation & passport · trading venues / OTFs · conduct of business · product governance · ASF supervision & sanctions.

## Operative regime (DD)

1. Map whether a trading entity needs SSIF authorisation vs only ANRE licence + OPCOM PEE registration.
2. Qualifying-holding / change-of-control approvals at ASF before closing on licensed intermediaries.
3. Do not conflate with [[Legea 24-2017 — emitenti instrumente financiare (notă)|L24/2017]] issuer/takeover rules.
