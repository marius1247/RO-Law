# Legea nr. 98/2016 privind achizițiile publice — Analysis

**Text:** *pending ingestion* — aligned with **Dir. 2014/24/EU** · **Platform:** SEAP/SICAP · **Hub:** [[_Regulatory Risk & Litigation Matrix]]

## Executive briefing

| Theme | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Core framework** | Award of public contracts and framework agreements by contracting authorities — non-discrimination, transparency, equal treatment |
| **SEAP / SICAP** | Mandatory electronic platform from publication to bid submission |
| **Dispute architecture** | CNSC administrative review **or** Bucharest Court of Appeal — guarantee deposit (*cauțiune*) often required |
| **Award default** | **Best price-quality ratio** (*cel mai bun raport calitate-preț*) — lifecycle costs, environmental performance |
| **Abnormally low tenders** | Bids <80% of estimated value trigger mandatory verification |

## Procurement award procedures

| Procedure | Use |
|---|---|
| **Open tender** (*licitație deschisă*) | Default for high-value contracts — direct SEAP submission |
| **Negotiated without notice** | Strictly exceptional — extreme urgency or single-source IP (art. 69) |

## Dispute resolution pipeline

```
Publication of award / tender documentation
        │
        ▼ (notification + guarantee deposit)
Option A: CNSC appeal          Option B: Court action
(fast-track admin review)      (Bucharest Court of Appeal)
        │                              │
        └──────────┬───────────────────┘
                   ▼
        Final appeal — Court of Appeal (irrevocable)
```

## Subcontracting & consortium rules

Transparency on beneficial owners, foreign subcontracting limits, and joint-venture (*asociere*) solidarity obligations.

## Strategic guidance for bidders

1. **Pre-litigation:** File clarification requests (*solicitări de clarificări*) during the tender window to build a procedural record for CNSC challenges.
2. **Guarantee deposit:** Post the mandatory litigation guarantee promptly when filing appeals — procedural rejection otherwise.
