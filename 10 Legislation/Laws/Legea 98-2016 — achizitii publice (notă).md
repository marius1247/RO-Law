## Thesis

Dir. 2014/24 transposition governing how contracting authorities award works/services/supplies via **SEAP/SICAP**. Critical for grid/EPC/public offtaker contracts and for M&A when the target holds active public awards. Challenge practice runs through **CNSC** or Bucharest Court of Appeal with *cauțiune* rules in L101.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Is it public procurement?
Contracting authority + covered contract type + thresholds. Energy network operators often sit under **L99 utilities** — flag early.

### Procedure choice
Open tender default; negotiated without prior notice only on narrow gates (urgency / exclusive rights — art. 69 area). Illegal direct award is a classic annulment vector.

### Abnormally low tenders
If bid < **~80%** of estimated value → mandatory verification (confirm consolidare). Failure to justify → rejection.

### Award criteria
Best price-quality / lifecycle / environmental criteria — relevant for green RES-adjacent buys.

### Challenge path
Award documents → **CNSC** (fast track) **or** CA București → further appeal. Short challenge windows (~10 days typical under L101 — **verify**). *Cauțiune* often required; missing it kills the challenge.

### M&A on contractor
Change of control / assignment / subcontracting consent on active awards and frameworks; capacity and UBO transparency on *asociere*.

### Bid-rigging
Cover bidding = [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21 art. 5]] + procurement exclusion risk.

## Obligations

**Authority:** publish, equal treatment, reason awards. **Bidder:** eligibility (DUAE/ESPD-style), bonds, clarification discipline, subcontracting disclosures.

## Enforcement

CNSC decisions; CA București; ANAP oversight; exclusion/debarment; criminal bid-rigging interface.

## Hard edges

- Dual CNSC/court path — strategy choice is irreversible in practice.
- Mid-contract modifications heavily constrained (Dir. 2014/24 art. 72 lineage).
- Clarification silence during tender can forfeit later arguments.
- CfD / state-aid auctions under [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)|HG 318]] are **not** classic L98 — don’t conflate.
- L98 vs L99 mix-up is common for Transelectrica/DSO procurement.
