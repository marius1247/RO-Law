# Legea nr. 31/1990 a societăților — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)]] · **Registration reform:** L265/2022 (ONRC) — amendments visible in this consolidare · **Tax companion:** [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]]

## What it is / scope

Romania's core **companies statute**: legal forms, constitutive act, capital, corporate governance (AGA / board / dualist system), modifications, mergers/divisions, dissolution and liquidation. It is the default corporate law for SRL and SA used by energy licensees, traders and project SPVs. Heavily amended (notably L441/2006 governance reform, L76/2012 terminology, **L265/2022** trade-register overhaul, L222/2023 cross-border mobility, L239/2025 SRL minimum capital note).

Prefer the portal consolidare in the vault text over older republicări when citing governance or registration articles.

## Structure at a glance

- **Titlul I** — General provisions (forms, liability)
- **Titlul II** — Formation (constitutive act, contributions, registration)
- **Titlul III** — Functioning (shares / părți sociale, AGA, administration — unitary & dualist, SRL rules)
- Later titles — modification of constitutive act, nullity, dissolution & liquidation, mergers/divisions, groups, sanctions, transitional

## Key provisions

- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Natural and legal persons may associate to form companies with legal personality for profit; seat in Romania ⇒ Romanian legal person.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 2|Art. 2]]** — Closed list of forms: SNC, SCS, **SA**, SCA, **SRL**.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 5|Art. 5]]** — Constitutive act (contract + statute, or single instrument). SRL may be formed by a single person (statute only). Authentic form mandatory for immovable contributions, SNC/SCS, or public-subscription SA. Date certain also by trade-register filing.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 8|Art. 8]]** — Mandatory contents of the SA / SCA constitutive act (founders, capital, share classes, first board/supervisory board, auditors, duration, dissolution, etc.). Identification data detail → art. 8¹.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 16|Art. 16]]** — Contributions: cash mandatory for every form; in-kind must be economically evaluable; receivables restricted; **labour/services cannot form or increase share capital** (partners in SNC/comanditați may contribute labour for profit share only).
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 111|Art. 111]]** — Ordinary general meeting of an SA: at least once a year within 5 months of financial year-end; approves financial statements and dividend; elects/revokes board or supervisory board and auditors; fixes remuneration; decides on pledge/lease/closure of units.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 137|Art. 137]]** — SA management: one or more directors (odd number); plural directors form the board. Audited SAs need at least three directors.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 143|Art. 143]]** — Delegation of executive management to directors (unitary system) — the hinge between board supervision and day-to-day management; pairs with art. 138¹ (majority non-executive board when delegation occurs).
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 153|Art. 153]]** (+ 153¹–153²⁴ cluster) — Dualist system (directorate + supervisory board), duties, conflicts, related-party limits, registration of appointments. High-density governance block for listed / large SAs.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 192|Art. 192]]** — SRL general meeting: absolute majority of associates **and** of share capital, unless the constitutive act provides otherwise.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 204|Art. 204]]** — Amendment of the constitutive act — registration and publication pathway (also used when publishing dissolution decisions under art. 232).
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 227|Art. 227]]** — Dissolution grounds: term expiry, impossibility/achievement of object, nullity, AGA decision, court for serious deadlock, bankruptcy, other legal/statutory causes.
- **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)#Articolul 233|Art. 233]]** — Dissolution opens liquidation (except merger/total division and other statutory cases); managers may not start new operations; legal personality continues for liquidation purposes until completion.

## How it connects

- **ONRC / registrul comerțului:** L265/2022 reforms (forms, EUID, electronic filing) — amend L31 articles throughout; ingest dedicated ONRC note when available.
- **Fiscal:** corporate tax and micro regimes in [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]]; procedure in [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (text) — Index|CPF]].
- **Energy SPVs:** licensed activities under [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] sit on L31 vehicles; change-of-control / shareholder fitness can matter for ANRE licensing (L123 art. 9).
- **MD stack (different architecture):** MD uses separate laws for SRL (L135/2007), SA (L1134/1997) and registration (L220/2007) rather than one Companies Act.
