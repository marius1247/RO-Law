# Legea 24/2017 — emitenți & operațiuni de piață — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 24-2017 — emitenti instrumente financiare (text)]] · **MiFID twin:** [[Legea 126-2018 — piete instrumente financiare (notă)|L126/2018]] · **Cluster hub:** [[Legea 208-2015 — piete de capital (notă)]] · **M&A:** [[_M&A Regulatory Compliance Checklist]]

## Thesis

Primary RO statute for **issuers of financial instruments and market operations** — disclosure, major holdings, and the **mandatory takeover bid** regime. This is the correct working text for listed-target M&A (not the misnamed L208 electoral file).

## Architecture

Issuers / prospectus / ongoing disclosure · major shareholding notifications · **mandatory takeover bid** (voting-rights thresholds) · market operations / buy-backs · ASF supervision interface.

## Operative regime (DD)

1. Crossing the regulated voting-rights threshold on a BVB issuer → mandatory takeover bid (confirm live % in consolidare — typically ~33%).
2. Acting in concert aggregates holdings.
3. MAR inside-information walls still apply in parallel (EU 596/2014).
4. Qualifying-holding / fit-and-proper approvals may also sit under ASF secondary rules for supervised entities.
