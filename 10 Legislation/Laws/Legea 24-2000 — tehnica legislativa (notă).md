# Legea nr. 24/2000 — norme de tehnică legislativă — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)]] · **Constitutional frame:** [[Constitutia Romaniei (text)|Constituția]]

> [!info] Currency
> Vault text is SOAP republication (`portal_id` 118116). Prefer a fresh portal consolidare if citing post-2010 amendments to drafting/publication rules.

## What it is / scope

The statute that tells Romanian drafters **how** to write, structure, amend, republish and put into force normative acts — laws, ordinances, government decisions, ministerial orders, and local acts. It is the meta-law behind clean article numbering, republications after heavy amendment, and the distinction between normative and individual acts. Essential when reconciling consolidări of L123/2012 or the tax codes.

## Structure at a glance

Rules on integration into the legal system, constitutive parts of an act, enacting terms, amendment/republication, entry into force, ministerial and local normative acts, and final/repeal provisions (arts. 1–85 in this file).

## Key provisions

- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 13|Art. 13]]** — Organic integration into the legal system: new drafts must fit existing hierarchy and terminology; avoid parallel regulation of the same matter; correlate with EU obligations where relevant.
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 17|Art. 17]]** — Legislative hygiene: when drafting, pursue repeal of obsolete or parallel norms (*asanarea legislației active*).
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 40|Art. 40]]** — Constitutive parts of a normative act: title; preamble where used; introductory formula; enacting part (*partea dispozitivă*); annexes.
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 44|Art. 44]]** — The enacting part is the actual normative content — articles, paragraphs, points — i.e. what vault `#Articolul N` anchors point to.
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 77|Art. 77]]** — Normative orders / instructions of ministers and other central bodies: when they may be issued and how they sit under primary law (the layer where many ANRE-adjacent ministry rules live; ANRE orders also track similar publication logic via OUG 33/2007 art. 5).
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 80|Art. 80]]** — Local public-administration normative acts: scope limited to organising local activities under the law.
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 83|Art. 83]]** — Local acts enter into force after bringing to public knowledge under the statutory rules (publication / display — check consolidare for e-publication practice).
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)#Articolul 85|Art. 85]]** — Repeal of prior legislative-technique decree on entry into force of this law.
