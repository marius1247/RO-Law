## Executive briefing

| Theme | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Administrative appeal** | Mandatory *contestație fiscală* within **45 days** of service — prerequisite to court litigation |
| **Immediate enforceability** | Tax decisions enforceable on issue; appeal does **not** auto-suspend collection |
| **Court suspension** | Requires guarantee (*cauțiune*) up to **20%** of contested liability (CPF art. 278 + Legea 554/2004) |
| **Prescription** | **5 years** general (from 1 July following fiscal year); **10 years** if tax evasion criminal element |
| **Digital enforcement** | RO e-Factura / SAF-T / e-TVA mismatch triggers audit — align ERP logs with D300 returns |

## What it is / scope

Romania's **tax administration procedure code**: how the tax authority and the taxpayer relate — definitions, fiscal legal relationship, general procedural rules, **tax registration**, assessment of claims, **tax inspection**, collection and enforcement, administrative challenges (*contestație*), mutual agreement for double taxation, international administrative cooperation, and sanctions. It is the operating manual for ANAF audits and for contesting a *decizie de impunere*. Substantive liability still comes from the [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]] (and special laws).

## Key provisions

### Opening rules

- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Definitions (tax authority, taxpayer/payer, fiscal claim, fiscal administrative act, etc.).
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 2|Art. 2]]** — Object and scope of the CPF.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 5|Art. 5]]** — Uniform application of tax legislation (links to Comisia fiscală centrală — see also Cod fiscal art. 6).
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 7|Art. 7]]** — Active role and conduct rules for the tax body (guidance, proportionality, hearing — the soft-law face of ANAF that matters in inspection files).

### Acts, registration, assessment

- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 46|Art. 46]]** — Content and reasoning of the fiscal administrative act — defect of reasoning is a classic annulment ground.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 82|Art. 82]]** — Obligation to register for tax purposes.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul V#Articolul 95|Art. 95]]** — **Decizia de impunere** — the assessment decision that crystallises the claim and starts challenge clocks.

### Inspection

- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 113|Art. 113]]** — Object of the tax inspection (verification of compliance with tax law for a defined period / taxes).
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VI#Articolul 118|Art. 118]]** — Inspection rules (rights of the taxpayer, duration, suspending events, draft report / *raport de inspecție fiscală* pathway). Read the whole Titlu VI before advising on an ongoing audit.

### Collection & interim measures

- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 157|Art. 157]]** — Outstanding fiscal obligations — starting point for enforcement and for certificates of fiscal clearance.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 213|Art. 213]]** — Conservatory attachment and seizure (*poprire* / *sechestru asigurător*) — used when collection is at risk before or during enforcement.

### Challenge

- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VIII#Articolul 268|Art. 268]]** — Possibility to contest fiscal administrative acts.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VIII#Articolul 272|Art. 272]]** — Competent challenge body.
- **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 — text — Titlul VIII#Articolul 277|Art. 277]]** — Suspension of the administrative challenge procedure — interaction with criminal files / related proceedings.

Standard path: reasoned *contestație* within the statutory term from communication of the act → administrative decision → administrative court. Terms and suspensory effect of security/payment must be checked on the **309359** consolidare.

## How it connects

- **Substantive:** [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]] — especially CF art. 11 (recharacterisation / TP) which ANAF operationalises through CPF inspection and assessment articles.
- **Corporate identity:** [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|L31/1990]] + ONRC data feed registration (Titlu IV).
- **EU / international:** Titlu X (automatic exchange, etc.); MAP Titlu IX for treaty cases; DAC/CRS amendments may post-date SOAP.
- **Energy clients:** inspection risk concentrates on VAT, TP on captive offtake, excise, and micro-vs-profit classification — procedure is always CPF even when the dispute is “about” L123 or L220 economics.
