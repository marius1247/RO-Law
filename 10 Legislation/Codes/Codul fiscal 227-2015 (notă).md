## What it is / scope

Romania's substantive tax code: who pays, what is taxable, how the tax is computed and when it is due for **profit tax, micro-enterprise tax, personal income tax, non-resident / permanent establishment tax, representation tax, VAT, excise, local taxes, construction tax**, plus the framework for **mandatory social contributions**. Administration (registration, assessment, audit, collection, challenges) lives in the [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (text) — Index|Codul de procedură fiscală]].

For energy and corporate work this is the daily statute behind SPV tax profiles, invoice VAT, excise on energy products, local building tax on plants, and payroll on O&M staff. Treaties override the Code where they conflict (art. 1(3)).

## Key provisions

### Titlul I — system rules

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — Scope: taxes and contributions listed in art. 2; taxpayers; calculation and payment; MFP norms. Administration → CPF. Treaties prevail. State-aid fiscal measures only under state-aid law.
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 2|Art. 2]]** — Catalogue of taxes (profit, micro, PIT, non-resident, representation, VAT, excise, local, construction) and social contributions (pension, health, unemployment, work accidents, wage-claim guarantee fund, etc.). Use this as the map of which Titlu applies.
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 7|Art. 7]]** — Common definitions (resident, Romania, royalties, fiscal value, withholding, stock-option plans, etc.). VAT and excise use their own definitional regimes inside Titluri VII–VIII.
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul I#Articolul 11|Art. 11]]** — Anti-abuse / substance toolkit: disregard or recharacterise transactions without economic purpose; artificial cross-border arrangements excluded from treaty benefits; **transfer pricing** at arm's length with OECD methods; inactive / cancelled VAT-number deduction blocks; VAT abuse-of-rights test; duty to follow CJEU case law in VAT and excise.

Also note art. 3 (fiscal principles, including predictability) and art. 4 (amendment lead times — often bypassed in practice by OUG, so verify Const. art. 115 discipline separately).

### Titlul II — profit tax (corporate)

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 13|Art. 13]]** — Who is a profit-tax payer (Romanian legal persons, foreign legal persons via PE / place of effective management, etc. — confirm current perimeter in 2026 consolidare).
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 19|Art. 19]]** — General computation rules (accounting profit adjusted for fiscal additions/deductions).
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul II#Articolul 25|Art. 25]]** — Deductible / limited / non-deductible expenses — the usual battlefield in ANAF audits (protocol, sponsorship, impairment, related-party charges).
- Related: art. 41 (declaration and payment) in Titlul II — calendar and forms move; check consolidare.

### Titlul III — micro-enterprise tax

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul III#Articolul 56|Art. 56]]** — Payment and tax returns for the micro regime. **Eligibility thresholds, rates and forced exits to profit tax have been rewritten repeatedly (2023–2026)** — treat SOAP text as structural only; verify against CF 311621/311622 before any client position.

### Titlul IV — personal income tax

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 60|Art. 60]]** — Personal exemptions (e.g. severe disability — categories evolve).
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 76|Art. 76]]** — Employment and assimilated income definition (benefits in kind, stock options interaction with art. 7).
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 91|Art. 91]]** — Investment income (dividends, interest, capital gains, etc.).
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul IV#Articolul 95|Art. 95]]** — Income from financial derivatives — relevant for traders and structured products.

### Titlul VII — VAT

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 269|Art. 269]]** — Taxable persons and economic activity.
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul VII#Articolul 286|Art. 286]]** — Taxable amount for domestic supplies of goods and services.

### Titlul IX — local taxes

- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 — text — Titlul IX#Articolul 491|Art. 491]]** — Indexation of local taxes — annual local council decisions matter as much as the Code.

## How it connects

- **Procedure & ANAF powers:** [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (text) — Index|CPF]] — registration, *decizie de impunere*, inspection, enforced collection, administrative challenge.
- **Corporate vehicles:** [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|L31/1990]] (SRL/SA); accounting rules feed art. 19 profit computation.
- **EU:** VAT Directive lineage and CJEU (explicitly art. 11(11)); ATAD / DAC6 / Pillar Two overlays may sit partly outside or in later amendments — confirm in 2026 consolidare.
- **Energy:** excise Title VIII for energy products; local tax on generation assets; profit/micro choice for project companies; CV invoice line under L220 is not a Cod fiscal tax but interacts with deductible cost analysis.
