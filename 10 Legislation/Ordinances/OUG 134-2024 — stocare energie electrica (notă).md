# OUG 134/2024 — electricity storage in L123 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[OUG 134-2024 — stocare energie electrica (text)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Amends:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]] · **Downstream:** [[ANRE — Norme stocare BESS 2025 (notă)|Ord. 56/2025]] · **Concepts:** [[Concept — BESS economics si tarifare stocare]] · [[Concept — BESS revenue stacking]]

## Thesis

Emergency ordinance that **embeds electricity storage in L123 primary law**: new actor definitions (storage service provider / storage operator / storage service), network-study duties for storage characteristics, and a **statutory ban on double-charging** stored energy for TL extraction, distribution, system services, green certificates and high-efficiency cogeneration contribution — with a parallel CHEAP / pumped-hydro concession track for national projects. Implemented operationally by [[ANRE — Norme stocare BESS 2025 (notă)|Ord. 56/2025]].

## Architecture

Amending OUG (not a stand-alone code). Main insertions into L123:

| Insertion | Role |
|---|---|
| Art. 3 pts. **48¹, 70¹, 107¹** + rewritten **121** | Storage vocabulary (provider / operator / service / storage of energy) |
| Art. **25(14)** | Network operators must account for storage characteristics in solution studies |
| Arts. **66³–66⁴** | Tariff exemptions + CHEAP concession / water / forestry derogations |
| Art. II (L220 amendments) | Gross final consumption / final consumer definitions exclude storage throughput from “final consumption” |

Policy drivers in the exposé: Dir. 2019/944 / Reg. 2019/943 alignment; PNRR storage target **≥240 MW / 480 MWh by 30 Jun 2026**; elimination of double taxation as bankability blocker.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### Who is a storage actor
- **Furnizor de servicii de stocare** (art. 3 pct. 48¹) — holds a storage installation used to provide storage services; absorbed energy is **not** final consumption.
- **Operator de servicii de stocare** (art. 3 pct. 70¹) — holds storage standalone or co-located with generation for later grid delivery; exception where storage feeds final customers via direct lines.
- **Serviciu de stocare** (art. 3 pct. 107¹) — absorb → store for limited time → redeliver to the network.
- **Stocare de energie** (art. 3 pct. 121 rewritten) — conversion / delayed use / reconversion (or other energy vector).

### Double-charging ban (core bankability rule)
[[OUG 134-2024 — stocare energie electrica (text)#Articolul 66^3|Art. 66³ L123]] exempts the storage installation operator, including CHEAP in pumping mode, from:
- **a)** transport tariff — **extraction component**, distribution, and system-services purchase (or components thereof) **as per ANRE rules**;
- **b)** green-certificate contribution;
- **c)** high-efficiency cogeneration contribution.

Operational detail (what “energie electrică stocată” means for billing, interim until CBA tariff methodology) → [[ANRE — Norme stocare BESS 2025 (notă)|Ord. 56/2025 arts. 3–5]].

### Connection / solution studies
[[OUG 134-2024 — stocare energie electrica (text)#Articolul 25|Art. 25(14)]] — TSO/DSO must consider storage characteristics when setting dimensioning regimes in solution studies for production / consumption sites. Interacts with [[ANRE — Regulament racordare EE 2013 (notă)|racordare regulament]] and [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]].

### CHEAP / national storage projects
[[OUG 134-2024 — stocare energie electrica (text)#Articolul 66^4|Art. 66⁴]] — Ministry of Energy can take over administration/concession of parts of permanent reservoir land for pumped-hydro; HG within 90 days for water-use contribution categories; forestry Code derogation for definitive deforestation without land compensation (tax + land value only). Art. III transitional duties on current administrators.

### L220 definition hygiene
Art. II rewrites L220 art. 2 lit. i) and k) so storage absorption/redelivery and process consumption in production/storage/transport/distribution are **not** “final consumer” volume for CV-scheme math — prevents CV obligation attaching to storage throughput.

## Enforcement

Primary sanctions remain those of L123 (licence / investigation chapters) and ANRE secondary acts. No self-standing offence catalogue in this OUG — the operative bite is **tariff / CV / cogeneration exemption** plus CHEAP administrative derogations.

## Hard edges

- Exemption applies to energy that is **stored and re-injected**; charging-leg losses / auxiliaries and non-stored offtake remain tariffed (Ord. 56 art. 4 vs art. 5).
- Co-located RES → storage → grid: confirm measured quantity is “energie electrică stocată” under Ord. 56 — not automatic inheritance of every RES support.
- Art. 66³ points to “conform reglementărilor ANRE” — always read Ord. 56 (interim) until permanent methodology.
- CHEAP track is **national-project infrastructure law**, not a shortcut for merchant BESS.
