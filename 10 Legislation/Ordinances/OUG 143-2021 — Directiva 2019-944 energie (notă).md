# OUG 143/2021 — Clean Energy Package into L123

## What it does

Transposes **Directive (EU) 2019/944** into **L123/2012 Title I**: consumer rights, supplier switching, dynamic-price contracts, independent aggregators, citizen energy communities (precursor definitions), and market-role adjustments post-liberalisation. Works together with **Reg. 2019/943** (implemented via ANRE market rules — still pending vault ingest).
