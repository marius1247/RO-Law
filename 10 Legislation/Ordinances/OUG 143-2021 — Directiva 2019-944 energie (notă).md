# OUG 143/2021 — Clean Energy Package into L123 — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[OUG 143-2021 — Directiva 2019-944 energie (text)]] · **Hub:** [[MOC — Energetică]] · **Amends:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (notă)|L123/2012]] · **EU:** Dir. (EU) 2019/944 · Reg. (EU) 2019/943 · **Downstream retail:** [[ANRE — Regulament furnizare EE 2023 (notă)]] · **Communities:** [[ANRE — Ordin 9-2026 Comunitati energie (notă)|Ord. 9/2026]]

## Thesis

Principal RO vehicle that **grafted Directive 2019/944 onto L123 Title I**: rewrote the definitions dictionary, expanded the licensing perimeter (storage, aggregation, trading), hard-wired consumer rights (switching platform, dynamic prices, independent aggregators, citizen energy communities), and forced ANRE secondary law to catch up. Still the interpretive key whenever a post-2021 L123 article looks “fourth-package”.

## Architecture

Large amending OUG (vault text ~1000+ lines). Structural blocks:

| Block | What it does |
|---|---|
| Art. 3 rewrite | Full Clean Energy Package vocabulary (aggregation, active customer, CEC, dynamic-price contract, storage, PRE, etc.) |
| Arts. 7¹–7⁶ | ANRE monitoring, ACER cooperation, comparison tool, dynamic-price supervision, national switching IT platform (POSF legal basis) |
| Art. 10 rewrite | Licence categories including co-located/standalone storage, aggregation, trading |
| Arts. 21–23 area | Market participation, balancing responsibility carve-outs, near-real-time trading |
| Consumer chapters | Switching, universal service / FUI split, active customer rights (later operationalised in Ord. 5/2023) |
| Touch on L220 | Distributed RES / prosumer encouragement hooks in exposé and selected amendments |

Adopted under infringement pressure (Commission letter 2021/0092) and PNRR energy reform conditionality.

## Operative regime (by problem)

### New market roles in the statute
- **Agregare / agregator independent** — art. 3 pts. 6–7: combine loads or generation for any market; independent aggregator not affiliated with the customer’s supplier.
- **Client activ** — art. 3 pct. 18: consume/store/sell/own generation or flexibility without that being primary business.
- **Comunitate de energie a cetățenilor** — art. 3 pct. 24: voluntary, locally controlled, non-profit-primary legal person; can do generation, distribution, supply, aggregation, storage, EV charging. Secondary rules → [[Concept — Comunitate de energie]] · Ord. 9/50/2026.
- **Contract cu prețuri dinamice** — art. 3 pct. 32: reflects spot (PZU/PI) at least at imbalance settlement granularity.
- **Stocare / instalație de stocare** — art. 3 pts. 57, 121 (further refined by [[OUG 134-2024 — stocare energie electrica (notă)|OUG 134/2024]]).

### Licensing perimeter
[[OUG 143-2021 — Directiva 2019-944 energie (text)#Articolul 10|Art. 10(2)]] — ANRE licences now expressly cover: generation (+ co-located storage), cogeneration (+ co-located storage), transmission & balancing, distribution, market operator, supply, **trading**, **aggregation**, **standalone storage**. Sub-1 MW generation/storage may operate without a licence but under ANRE rules (art. 10(5)). See [[Concept — Licență în energetică]].

### Consumer switching & digitalisation
- Art. **7³** — ANRE information campaigns on free supplier switching.
- Art. **7⁴** — independent offer-comparison tool (or trust-mark regime).
- Art. **7⁶** — national IT platform for supplier switching (legal parent of **POSF**); licence holders must feed data; ANRE may outsource operation to a non-affiliated provider.
- Retail operational detail → [[ANRE — Regulament furnizare EE 2023 (notă)]] arts. 14, 20 (POSF / switching).

### Balancing responsibility carve-out
Art. 21(3) area — prosumers with installed generation **< 400 kW** are not financially responsible for imbalances; responsibility sits with their supplier (ANRE detail). Cross-link [[Concept — PRE si responsabilitate de echilibrare]].

### Market design hooks
Art. 23 rewrite — bilateral + organised markets in all timeframes; trade as close to real time as possible; minimum offer size ≤ 500 kW on DAM/ID to admit demand response, storage and small RES; voluntary market participation.

## Definitions

Treat the OUG’s art. 3 rewrite as the **controlling dictionary** for Title I until a later consolidare moves the text. Always cite the L123 consolidare article number (vault: [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)]]), not the OUG article alone.

## Obligations

| Actor | Duty introduced / hardened |
|---|---|
| ANRE | Comparison tool, switching platform, dynamic-price monitoring, ACER cooperation, secondary rules |
| Suppliers | Switching without obstructive clauses; dynamic-price offers when >200k customers + SMI (furnizare Reg.); data for POSF |
| Aggregators | Licence (unless exempt); no supplier-consent veto on aggregation contracts (consumer rights chapter) |
| Generators / storage | Licensing thresholds; market participation rules |
| Network operators | TPA / connection duties unchanged in principle; storage characteristics later reinforced by OUG 134 |

## Enforcement

L123 investigation / offence chapters continue to apply. Infringement context matters for interpretation: OUG was the emergency transposition vehicle after the Feb 2021 reasoned letter — courts and ANRE tend to read ambiguities toward Dir. 2019/944 conformity.

## Hard edges

- **Amend-in-place pattern**: article numbers in advice must be checked against the current L123 consolidare (vault id `307058`), not the 2021 OUG alone.
- Gas Title II was **not** the focus — do not assume 2019/944 concepts automatically migrate to gas.
- Citizen energy community definition here is the L123 baseline; CESR (renewable communities) under OUG 163 / Ord. 9 is a **parallel** track — see [[Concept — Comunitate de energie]].
- Storage tariff double-charging fix came later (OUG 134) — OUG 143 put storage in the licence perimeter but did not finish the tariff story.
