# OUG nr. 33/2007 — organizarea și funcționarea ANRE — Analysis

**Raw text:** [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)]] · **Sector statute:** [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] · **Constitutional OUG frame:** [[Constitutia Romaniei (text)#Articolul 115|Const. art. 115]]

> [!info] Currency
> Portal consolidare Afis id `257158` (notes through L221/2022). Institutional amendments (parliamentary appointment of the Regulation Committee) are material — do not rely on pre-2020 summaries of who appoints the president.

## What it is / scope

The organic-style statute (in OUG form, approved/amended by successive laws) that creates **ANRE** as an autonomous administrative authority: financing from own revenues, leadership, **Comitetul de reglementare**, types of ANRE acts, general objectives, and detailed electricity / gas competences. L123/2012 names ANRE as competent authority (art. 7) but **this OUG** is where independence, budget, appointments and the catalogue of regulatory instruments live.

EU parallel: NRA independence requirements under `EU Law/10 Legislation/Directives/Dir 2019-944 — electricity market` and the gas directive lineage (Dir. 2009/73). MD parallel: ANRE competences embedded mainly in L164/2025 / L108/2016 rather than a separate organisation OUG.

## Structure at a glance

Arts. 1–11 cluster: status & parliamentary reporting (1); financing (2); president / vice-presidents (3); Regulation Committee (4); ANRE acts & consultation (5); advisory council (6); staff (7); general objectives (8); EE & cogeneration powers (9); gas powers (10); relations with third parties / ACER (11).

## Key provisions

- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 1|Art. 1]]** — ANRE: autonomous administrative authority with legal personality, under parliamentary control, financed entirely from own revenues, decisionally / organisationally / functionally independent. Object: draft, approve and monitor binding national regulations for electricity, heat and gas markets. Annual activity report to Parliament by 31 July (debated, not voted); also sent to Government, President, **ACER**, Commission, Competition Council.
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 2|Art. 2]]** — Own-revenue financing: licence/authorisation/attestation fees, annual contributions from regulated operators, international funds. Tariffs/contributions set yearly by ANRE president order (from 2020). Own budget approval; Court of Accounts review limited to economic-financial operations.
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 3|Art. 3]]** — Leadership: one president + two vice-presidents; representation rules; professional eligibility (technical / economic / legal; 10 years experience; 5 years management in energy or related).
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 4|Art. 4]]** — **Comitetul de reglementare** (7 members: 4 non-executive + president + 2 VPs). Appointment/revocation by Parliament in joint session on specialised-committee proposal (L221/2022). Five-year mandate, once renewable. Independence / no industry holdings or management roles; solidary responsibility except recorded dissent. Approves the core regulatory output.
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 5|Art. 5]]** — ANRE instruments: licences/authorisations; regulated prices & methodologies; technical & commercial regulations; approvals/endorsements of operator documents; attestations; other norms. General regulations → **orders published in MO Partea I**; mandatory debate in the Regulation Committee for core categories; binding until final court judgment to the contrary. Art. 5(7) ceased effects after CCR Decision 136/2015.
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 8|Art. 8]]** — General objectives: competitive EU internal markets, regional integration, cross-border trade, consumer-oriented secure networks, RES & distributed generation access, efficiency incentives, consumer protection & vulnerable customers, transparency.
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 9|Art. 9]]** — Electricity & cogeneration competences: licensing; tariff methodologies; network & system tariffs; FUI/universal-service related price rules (as per primary law); framework contracts; technical/commercial regs; interconnection access; control & sanctions; pre-contractual dispute resolution; monitoring list (congestion, 10-year network plan, unbundling accounts, connection terms, wholesale transparency, etc.).
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)#Articolul 10|Art. 10]]** — Gas competences: decisions/orders/avis; market investigations (incl. with Competition Council); licensing & personnel authorisations; technical safety norms; TPA / connection regulations; storage dispatch rules; regulated price/tariff methodologies; monitoring of market, access, congestion, account unbundling (incl. biogas/biomethane/LNG).
