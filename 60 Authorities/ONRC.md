# ONRC — Oficiul Național al Registrului Comerțului

**Level:** public institution under the Ministry of Justice (trade-register system)  
**Basis:** **Legea 265/2022** regarding the trade register and for amending other acts *(to ingest as working text)*; company-law interface in [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|L31/1990]].

## Mandate
Keeps the **Registrul comerțului**: registration of companies and other traders, branches, amendments to constitutive acts, representation data, and related publicity. Registration is **constitutive** for companies — legal personality arises on registration, not on signature of the constitutive act.

Three jobs (do not conflate):
1. **Creates** legal personality  
2. **Publicises** — third-party reliance on registered data  
3. **Controls** — limited legality check at entry  

## Why counterparties care
Administrator authority is verified against the register. Unregistered changes generally cannot be opposed to good-faith third parties. Dissolution and liquidation also run through ONRC procedures.

## Acts / outputs
- Registration decisions / certificates  
- Register excerpts (*informatii*) used in KYC and contracting  
- Normative secondary rules under L265/2022 *(map when ingested)*
