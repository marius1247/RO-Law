# ANAF — Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală

**Level:** specialised body of the central public administration (under the Ministry of Finance)  
**Basis:** organisation acts of the Government / MF; substantive and procedural mandates in [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]] and [[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (text) — Index|CPF]].

## Mandate
Administers national tax claims: registration, assessment, **fiscal control**, collection, enforcement, and the administrative channel for **contestații** against fiscal administrative acts (CPF Titlul VIII). Operates taxpayer services (SPV), risk analysis, and antifraud structures as organised internally.

## Position in the dispute chain
1. Control / assessment decision  
2. **Administrative contestation to ANAF** — mandatory first stage; short deadlines  
3. Judicial review  

Enforcement (account seizure, attachments) can move quickly; suspension is a separate application.
