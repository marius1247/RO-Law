# Concept — Societate cu răspundere limitată (SRL)

**RO:** societate cu răspundere limitată (SRL) · **EN:** limited liability company

## Definition
The closed company form under **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|Legea 31/1990]]** in which members’ liability is limited to their contributions, ownership is expressed as **părți sociale** (participations, not securities), and the structure is designed for closely held ownership. Romania’s default vehicle for SMEs, subsidiaries, JVs, and holding companies.

## Core features
| Feature | SRL |
|---|---|
| Ownership unit | *parte socială* |
| Transfer | Restricted by default (pre-emption, formalities, register update) |
| Organs | General meeting + administrator(s); board optional / simplified |
| Disclosure | Lighter than SA |
| Register | [[ONRC]] |

## Contrast
- vs **[[Concept — Societate pe actiuni|SA]]** — SA uses *acțiuni*, heavier governance, capital-markets path
- vs **MD SRL (L135/2007)** — same economic function; Moldova abolished the historic minimum capital floor; RO rules live in L31 consolidare (verify before advising)

## Formation & publicity
Constitutive act → registration with [[ONRC]] under **L265/2022** is constitutive. Third parties rely on register data for representation and existence. → [[Societăți & guvernanță — synthesis]]

## Tax interface
SRL is compatible with **impozit pe profit** or, where criteria are met, the **micro** regime (Cod fiscal Titlul III). Form choice and tax regime must be modelled together. → [[Fiscalitate — synthesis]] · [[Concept — Impozit pe profit]]
