# Concept — Contract pentru diferență (CfD)

## Definition

A **two-way contract for difference** under which a RES generator receives (or pays) the difference between a **strike price** and a **market reference price** for metered eligible production. In Romania the scheme sits in [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)|HG 318/2024]], with project contracts (Anexa eolian/solar) and billing/collection contracts under [[ANRE — Ordin 52-2024 Contracte-cadru CfD (notă)|Ord. 52/2024]]. Distinct from the consumer-side [[Concept — Contributie CfD|contribuție CfD]] that funds the liquidity pool.

## Operative points

- **Bidirectional:** generator paid when reference < strike; pays when reference > strike (subject to negative-price / eligibility carve-outs in HG 318).
- **Actors:** Ministry of Energy (scheme), OPCOM (Contraparte CfD), Transelectrica (scheme operator interfaces), ANRE (contribution rate + frameworks).
- **Reference price:** typically PZU/ID-weighted construct in the Anexa contract (Clauza 7) — confirm live annex.
- **Not** the legacy [[Concept — Certificat verde|CV]] scheme (L220 closed accreditation window).
