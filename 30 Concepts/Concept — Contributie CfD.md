# Concept — Contribuție CfD

**RO:** contribuția aferentă contractelor pentru diferență · **EN:** CfD scheme contribution (lei/kWh)

## Definition

> A regulated **lei/kWh surcharge** on electricity consumed in Romania that funds administration (and scheme corrections) of the low-carbon **Contracts for Difference** support mechanism under [[HG 318-2024 — cadru general CfD (notă)|HG 318/2024]]. Built by [[ANRE — Metodologie contributie CfD 2024 (text)#Articolul 5|Ord. 51/2024]] and billed through [[ANRE — Ordin 52-2024 Contracte-cadru CfD (notă)|Ord. 52/2024]] framework contracts with OPCOM as CfD Counterparty.

## How it works

```
ANRE sets CSTA_CfD (lei/kWh) = admin costs (± corrections, ≤5% profit) / forecast RO consumption
        │
        ▼
Suppliers / self-supplying producers (+ storage per Ord. 52) collect from consumers
        │
        ▼
Monthly report → OPCOM invoice → CfD liquidity fund → Transelectrica (Scheme Operator share)
```

- **Annual set:** by 20 Dec for next year (art. 5).
- **H2 revision:** if >2% drift, new rate from 1 Jul (arts. 8–11).
- Invoice must show CfD as a **separate line** (art. 23).

## Contrast with other charges

| Charge | Funds | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| **Contribuție CfD** | CfD admin + corrections | Ord. 51/52 · HG 318 |
| **CV quota cost** | RES certificate scheme | L220 · Ord. 96/81 |
| **TG/TL/SS/TD** | Network / reserves | Ord. 68/67/116 rate Ordine |

Not the CfD **strike price differential** owed between beneficiary and Counterparty — that is the CfD contract itself.
