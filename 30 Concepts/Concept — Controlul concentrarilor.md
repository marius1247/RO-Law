# Concept — Controlul concentrărilor

## Definition

Romanian **merger control**: a lasting change of control (sole or joint) that meets the [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)|L21/1996]] notification thresholds must be **cleared by Consiliul Concurenței before implementation**. Standstill applies; gun-jumping and below-threshold **call-in** are live risks for energy M&A.

Primary note: [[Legea 21-1996 — concurenta (notă)]].

## Operative points

- **Control**, not deal value — minority vetoes / board seats can create joint control.
- **Standstill** until clearance; clean teams for pre-close planning.
- **Call-in** may catch non-notifiable deals in sensitive markets (energy/infra).
- Parallel tracks: ANRE [[Concept — Licență în energetică|licence]] change-of-control ≠ RCC clearance ≠ ASF/BNR approvals.
