# Concept — TVA

**RO:** taxa pe valoarea adăugată (TVA) · **EN:** value added tax

> [!warning] Currency
> Standard/reduced rates and exemption lists are amended often. Working Cod fiscal text is **lagging**. Re-verify before use. → [[Currency ledger]]

## Definition
A general, indirect consumption tax on supplies of goods and services and on imports, governed by **Titlul VII** of [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]]. Built on the **EU VAT Directive** model: output tax on sales minus input tax on purchases; registered businesses are collectors, not ultimate bearers.

## Rate families *(verify live)*
| Family | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Full charge |
| **Reduced** | Listed supplies |
| **0% / exempt with credit** | Exports & assimilated — input VAT recoverable |
| **Exempt without credit** | No output VAT; **no** input recovery |

## Zero-rated vs exempt — the distinction that costs money
- **Zero-rated** — taxable at nil; input VAT **recoverable**
- **Exempt** — outside the charge; input VAT **not recoverable** (embedded cost)

Clients routinely treat them as equivalent. They are not.

## Registration — a strategic decision
Threshold-based mandatory registration with voluntary registration below threshold. Interacts with the **micro** profit-tax track and with B2B vs B2C customer mix. Model together with [[Concept — Impozit pe profit]].

## Mechanics that decide audits
- Chargeable event / time of supply
- **Valid invoice** — no invoice, no deduction (most disputes are evidential)
- Reverse charge on specified cross-border / domestic cases
- Import VAT at the border, then deductible
- Partial exemption apportionment
- Refund timing for export/investment-heavy businesses

## Notes / conclusions
> TVA is administratively the heaviest tax for most RO businesses. Recurring failure modes: **defective invoices** destroying deductions, and **slow refunds** creating financing cost that never appears in the model.
