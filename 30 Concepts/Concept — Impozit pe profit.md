# Concept — Impozit pe profit

**RO:** impozit pe profit · **EN:** corporate income tax / profit tax

> [!warning] Currency
> Rates, micro thresholds, and deductible-expense rules move via OUGs and annual fiscal measures. Working Cod fiscal text is **lagging**. Re-verify before use. → [[Currency ledger]]

## Definition
The tax on taxable profit of legal persons, governed by **Titlul II** of [[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal (L227/2015)]]. Distinct from the **microenterprise tax** (Titlul III), which taxes turnover for qualifying entities.

## Architecture
| Track | Titlu | Base |
|---|---|---|
| **Profit tax** | II | Taxable profit (accounting profit ± fiscal adjustments) |
| **Micro tax** | III | Turnover / qualifying revenues (if criteria met) |

Choosing between Titlul II and III is a structuring decision linked to VAT status, headcount, and ownership — remodel after every consolidare.
