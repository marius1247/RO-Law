# Concept — Unbundling

**RO:** separarea activităților / unbundling · **EN:** unbundling

## Definition
The mandatory **separation of network activities** (transmission, distribution) **from competitive activities** (generation, supply, trading) inside a vertically integrated undertaking. Transposed from the EU energy packages into [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] (both electricity and gas titles).

## Why it exists
A firm that owns the wires and also sells energy can disadvantage rivals through access terms, connection queues, investment choices, and cross-subsidy from the regulated network business. Structural separation removes the incentive instead of endlessly policing conduct.

## The ladder of models
1. **Accounting unbundling** — separate accounts per activity
2. **Functional / management unbundling** — independent decision-making and staff
3. **Legal unbundling** — separate legal entity; ownership may remain common
4. **Ownership unbundling (OU)** — no control in generation/supply; EU preferred for TSOs  
   Alternatives: **ISO** / **ITO** with compensating independence safeguards

**Certification** is the enforcement gate: a TSO is designated only after the regulator certifies compliance; third-country control triggers an additional security-of-supply test.

## Where it bites in Romania
- **Electricity** — Transelectrica as TSO; distribution groups with supply affiliates historically
- **Gas** — TSO/DSO perimeter and historic Vertically Integrated Undertaking structures
- Live questions are certification conditions, compliance officers, and **corporate perimeter** after group reorganisations

## Corporate-law mechanics
Executed through ordinary company law: spin-offs, contributions in kind, transfers of going concerns, shareholder-agreement changes. Analysis lives in **two** places. → [[Societăți & guvernanță — synthesis]] · [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|L31/1990]]
