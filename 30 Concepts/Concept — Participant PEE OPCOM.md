# Concept — Participant PEE OPCOM

**RO:** participant la piețele organizate de energie electrică (PEE) · **EN:** OPCOM organised electricity market participant

## Definition

> A legal person admitted by **OPCOM** (as OPEED) under the [[OPCOM — Procedura inregistrare PEE rev 17 (text)|Registration Procedure Rev. 17]] to trade on organised electricity markets after executing the **Convenție de Participare** and posting required guarantees. Licensing from [[ANRE]] is a prerequisite where L123 requires it; registration is the **market-access gate**, not a substitute for the licence.

## How it works

```
ANRE licence / authorisation (as required)
        │
        ▼ OPCOM registration file (Rev. 17)
        │
        ▼ Convenție de Participare + annexes
        │
        ▼ Guarantees (PZU/PI procedure) → live products (PZU, IDM, forward…)
```

- Product election is per annex; day-ahead ops in [[OPCOM — PO-PZU Rev7 2025 (notă)]].
- PRE / physical notification changes: [[OPCOM — Procedura NF PZU-PRE 2015 (notă)]] · [[Concept — PRE si responsabilitate de echilibrare]].
- Bilateral / OTC PPAs sit partly outside OPCOM but still under residual [[ANRE — Ordin 25-2004 Cod comercial piata angro EE (notă)|Cod comercial]] bilateral rules + L123.

> For DD: sequence is **licence → OPCOM registration → guarantees → trading**. Skipping OPCOM registration while holding a licence blocks organised-market routes; bilaterals may still be available under Cod comercial / L123.
