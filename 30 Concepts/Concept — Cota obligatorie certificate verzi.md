# Concept — Cotă obligatorie certificate verzi

**RO:** cotă anuală obligatorie de achiziție de certificate verzi · **EN:** mandatory green-certificate purchase quota

## Definition

> The **quota** is the number of [[Concept — Certificat verde|green certificates]] an obligated party must hold per MWh of obligated final consumption, set under [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|L220/2008]] via [[ANRE — Metodologie quota certificate verzi 2022 (text)#Articolul 5|Ord. 96/2022]].
>
> There are always **two** numbers in play: the **estimated** quota (December t−1 — drives quarterly fines) and the **final** quota (by 1 March t+1 — drives AFM substitute payment).

## How it works

```
Dec t−1:  estimated quota Ordin  ──► quarterly compliance 2026
Mar t+1:  final quota Ordin      ──► annual close + AFM if short
```

**2026 estimated:** **0,499387 CV/MWh** — [[ANRE — Ordin 81-2025 Quota certificate verzi 2026 (notă)|Ord. 81/2025]].

Math identity (estimated): `(ICV_€ × FX) / PCSCV_avg_price`. Higher GC spot prices → lower quota for the same statutory bill-impact ceiling.

### Extra compliance layer — PCSCV 50%
At least **50%** of the residual quarterly/annual need (after privileged pre-OUG 24/2017 bilaterals and self-transfers) must be bought on the anonymous spot GC market — Ord. 96/2022 arts. 11, 26.

## Governing provisions

- [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|L220/2008]] arts. 4, 8, 10, 12, 30
- [[ANRE — Metodologie quota certificate verzi 2022 (text)#Articolul 5|Ord. 96/2022 arts. 5–8, 10–16, 20–30]]
- [[ANRE — Ordin 81-2025 Quota certificate verzi 2026 (text)|Ord. 81/2025]]
- Exemptions: L123 art. 73¹(7); HG 495/2014
