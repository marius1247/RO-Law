# Concept — Licență în energetică

**RO:** licență · **EN:** licence / sectoral authorisation

## Definition
An administrative act by which [[ANRE]] authorises a person to **carry on a regulated activity** in the electricity, gas, or (where applicable) thermal energy sector. It attaches to the *activity*, not to a site — site attachment is [[Concept — Racordare la rețea|connection]].

## Licensable activities *(illustrative)*
Electricity: generation, transmission, distribution, supply, and specified market/operator functions. Gas: transport, distribution, storage, supply. Always check the live L123 list — trading, aggregation, and storage sit at the edges and move with EU packages / OUGs.

## How it works
- **Grant** — conditions and procedure in L123 + ANRE regulations; fees under [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]] financing model
- **Content** — licence **conditions** are enforceable in their own right
- **Modification / transfer** — regulated; change of control often triggers notification or re-approval
- **Suspension / withdrawal** — terminal sanctions; supplier withdrawal triggers [[Concept — Furnizor de ultimă instanță|FUI]] machinery

## Why it is the first question in any energy mandate
The licence is the **entry gate and the leash**. It determines whether the client can operate at all, and it is the instrument through which market rules, network codes, tariffs, and PSOs are made enforceable against them. A licensing defect contaminates everything built on top.

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] — licensing chapters (EE and gas)
- [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]] — ANRE competence & fees
- Individual licence Decizii — **out of vault scope**; pull from ANRE when advising a named operator
