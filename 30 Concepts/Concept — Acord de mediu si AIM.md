# Concept — Acord de mediu și AIM

## Definition

The environmental permit spine for industrial / energy projects in Romania:

1. **EIA / evaluare a impactului** (screening → study → public consultation) — specialised rules largely in **Legea 292/2018** under the umbrella of [[OUG 195-2005 — protectia mediului (notă)|OUG 195/2005]].
2. ***Acord de mediu*** — required **before** building permit / construction of projects subject to EIA.
3. ***Autorizație integrată de mediu (AIM)*** (or applicable environmental authorisation tier) — required **before** commercial operation for IED / listed installations.

## Operative points (energy DD)

- **Acord ≠ AIM** — build vs operate; both may be conditions precedent in financing.
- Public-consultation defects are the usual NGO annulment vector.
- M&A: verify permit holder name, amendments on operator change, GNM/APM suspension history; historical contamination → *bilanț de mediu* / successor liability.
- Do not conflate CSRD/ESG reporting with permitting.
