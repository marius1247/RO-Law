# Concept — Comunitate de energie

**RO:** comunitate de energie din surse regenerabile (CESR) / comunitate de energie a cetățenilor (CEC) · **EN:** renewable energy community / citizens’ energy community

## Definition

> A legal entity meeting the EU/RO criteria for collective energy activity — **CESR** (RED II / [[OUG 163-2022 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|OUG 163/2022]]) or **CEC** (electricity market directive / [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] art. 63¹) — that obtains **operational status** by registration in ANRE’s national register ([[ANRE — Ordin 50-2026 Procedura registru comunitati (text)#Articolul 4|Ord. 50/2026]]) and may trade on wholesale markets / offer storage flexibility under [[ANRE — Ordin 9-2026 Reguli comunitati energie (text)#Articolul 6|Ord. 9/2026]].

## How it works

### Eligibility (cumulative — Ord. 50 art. 4(3))
- ≥ **5** members (PF, local admin, SMEs, associations/foundations as applicable)
- Open & voluntary participation; effective member control; autonomy
- Primary aim: community economic / social / environmental benefits
- **Same DSO** for members’ consumption points (CESR also: production units on same DSO)
- Smart meters installed & integrated for all members
- Internal operating regulation adopted
- Post-registration: **180 days** to reach full conformity; status tags (`în conformare` / `conform` / …) are reputational, not stripping operational status (art. 4(5–7))

### Market participation (Ord. 9)
- Registered community = **market participant** (Reg. 2019/943) — may sell surplus after member needs, buy for members, join balancing as BSP, conclude bilaterals (art. 6).
- Needs appropriate **ANRE licence** (generation, supply, trader, aggregation, storage…) unless licence exemption (<1 MW connected generation/storage, or black-start own-safety units) — arts. 7–8.
- Storage of community **and/or members** may provide services to DSOs (arts. 2, later chapters).

### Register gate
Apply within **30 days** of legal incorporation (Ord. 50 shell art. 3); late filing still allowed but **recorded**. Register operational from first day of month after Ord. 50 entry into force.
