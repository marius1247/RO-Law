# Companies & corporate governance — synthesis

The reasoning layer for Romanian company law. Act inventory: [[MOC — Societăți & Guvernanță]].

## 1. The architecture

Romanian company law is **two-layered for forms, plus a registration statute**:

| Layer | Instrument | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Special (forms) | **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|Legea 31/1990]]** | SRL, SA, and other company forms: formation, capital, organs, dissolution |
| Procedural / register | **Legea 265/2022** (ONRC — to ingest) | Trade register: constitutive registration, publicity, branches, e-procedures |
| Background private law | **Codul civil** (L287/2009) | Legal persons, obligations, representation — fills gaps |

Contrast with Moldova’s **split** special laws: SRL = L135/2007, SA = L1134/1997, registration = L220/2007. Romania kept forms in **one** companies act (L31) and modernised the register via L265/2022.

## 2. SRL vs SA — choosing the form

| | **SRL** | **SA** |
|---|---|---|
| Statute | L31/1990 (SRL chapters) | L31/1990 (SA chapters) |
| Ownership unit | *parte socială* — participation | *acțiune* — security |
| Transfer | Restricted by default (pre-emption, formalities, register) | Freer in principle; dematerialised shares / register mechanics |
| Governance | AGA + administrator(s); board optional / simplified | AGA + board or dual structure options; heavier disclosure for public SAs |
| Capital | Flexible — founders set; watch any live statutory floors / contributions in kind rules | Higher formalisation; public SA / listed overlays |
| Register | [[ONRC]] | [[ONRC]] + capital-markets overlay (ASF) if public |
| Typical use | SMEs, subsidiaries, JVs, holdings | Banks, insurers, listed entities, large utilities |

→ [[Concept — Societate cu raspundere limitata]] · [[Concept — Societate pe actiuni]] · [[Concept — Capital social]]

**Practical read.** SRL is the default vehicle. SA is chosen when regulation or capital-markets access requires it, or when share transferability and institutional governance are the point.

## 3. Registration — how a company exists

State registration with **[[ONRC]]** under **L265/2022** is **constitutive**: the legal person comes into being on registration, not on signature of the constitutive act. The register:

1. **Creates** legal personality  
2. **Publicises** — third parties rely on registered data  
3. **Controls** — limited legality check at entry  

Administrator authority is verified against the register. Changes to the constitutive act and representation take effect on registration. Dissolution / liquidation also run through the register.

MD parallel: ASP + L220/2007 — same three jobs, different institutional brand.

## 4. The governance triangle

Same three-way tension as everywhere: **owners / managers / creditors**.

- **Owners → managers:** reserved matters for the general meeting, appointment/removal, fiduciary duties, related-party controls.
- **Majority → minority:** qualified majorities, pre-emption, withdrawal/appraisal, information rights, challenge to resolutions.
- **Company → creditors:** capital maintenance, distribution limits, net-assets triggers, directors’ duties near insolvency (Insolvency Law 85/2014 — to deepen).

## 5. Where energy meets company law

Unbundling a vertically integrated utility is executed as **corporate reorganisation**: spin-off of the network business, contribution of a going concern, governance ring-fencing. The regulatory obligation sits in [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]]; the mechanics sit here. → [[Concept — Unbundling]] · [[Energetică — synthesis]]
