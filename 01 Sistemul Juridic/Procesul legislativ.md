# Procesul legislativ (the law-making process)

How a normative act comes into being under the [[Constitutia Romaniei (text)|Constitution]] and [[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)|Legea 24/2000]] (legislative technique).

## Laws (legi)
1. **Initiative** — Government, MPs, and (for certain matters) other subjects of legislative initiative.
2. **Drafting** — structure, impact assessment, and technique rules under L24/2000.
3. **Avis / consultation** — Economic and Social Council and other mandatory avis where required; public consultation for many Government drafts.
4. **Parliamentary procedure** — bicameral; **organic** laws need the majority of each Chamber’s members (Const. art. 76); ordinary laws need a majority of members present.
5. **Mediation / reflection** — disagreement between Chambers resolved under constitutional rules.
6. **Promulgation** — by the President (who may return once or refer to the Constitutional Court).
7. **Publication** — [[Monitorul Oficial]] → **entry into force** (usually three days after publication unless otherwise provided — Const. art. 78).

## Ordonanțe de urgență (the RO shortcut)
Under **Const. art. 115**, the Government may adopt an **[[Concept — Ordonanță de urgență|OUG]]** in extraordinary situations whose regulation cannot be postponed:

1. **Adoption by Government** — urgency reasoning in the preamble is constitutionally material.
2. **Immediate submission to Parliament** for approval / rejection.
3. **Publication in MO** — produces effects as law pending parliamentary decision.
4. **Parliamentary approval law** — may approve as-is, amend, or reject; rejection raises complex transitional issues for acts already performed.

> [!warning] Do not treat an OUG as “provisional soft law”
> Until rejected, it binds like a statute. Energy market-design and tax OUGs are the main reason Romania’s secondary layer moves faster — and less predictably — than Moldova’s.

## Ordonanțe & hotărâri
- **Ordonanțe** (simple) — under an enabling law (art. 115) or as Government normative acts within competence.
- **HG** — organisation and execution of laws (art. 108); cannot amend a statute.

## Secondary / regulator layer
Parliament and Government set the framework; **[[ANRE]]**, ministries, and agencies issue **Ordine / regulamente** under express enabling articles. Trace every operational duty up the delegation chain — same mental model as MD HANRE acts. → [[Energetică — sinteza sectorului]]
