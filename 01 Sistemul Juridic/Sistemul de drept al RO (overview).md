# The Romanian legal system — overview

A one-page orientation before diving into any domain.

## Family
Romania is a **continental / civil-law** jurisdiction. Codified written acts are the primary source; case law is not formally binding precedent, though **Curtea Constituțională** decisions are binding *erga omnes* on the points decided, and High Court (*ÎCCJ*) rulings in the interest of the law / preliminary rulings on legal questions are strongly persuasive. Legal reasoning runs from general codes to special laws (*lex specialis derogat legi generali*).

## The main pillars
- **[[Constitutia Romaniei (text)|Constituția]]** (1991, **republicată 2003** after the L429/2003 revision) — supreme law.
- **Codul civil** (L287/2009) — private-law backbone.
- **Codul de procedură civilă**, **Codul penal**, **Codul de procedură penală**, **Codul muncii**, **Codul administrativ** (OUG 57/2019), plus sectoral codes.
- **[[Codul fiscal 227-2015 (text) — Index|Codul fiscal]]** (L227/2015) and **[[Codul de procedura fiscala 207-2015 (text) — Index|Codul de procedură fiscală]]** (L207/2015) — taxation substance and procedure.
- Company law: **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|Legea 31/1990]]** (forms) + **Legea 265/2022** (ONRC / trade register — to ingest).
- Energy: **[[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|Legea 123/2012]]** (EE + gas, two titles) and **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|Legea 220/2008]]** (green certificates).

## The OUG peculiarity
Unlike Moldova’s cleaner Parliament→Government cascade, Romania makes heavy use of **[[Concept — Ordonanță de urgență|ordonanțe de urgență]]** under **Const. art. 115**. An OUG has force of law pending parliamentary approval, can amend statutes, and is the ordinary vehicle for urgent market-design changes in energy and tax. Treat OUGs as primary legislation for practical purposes — and track their approval / rejection status. → [[Ierarhia actelor normative]] · [[Procesul legislativ]]

## Key organising laws (non-code)
- **[[Legea 24-2000 — tehnica legislativa (text)|Legea 24/2000]]** — legislative technique (drafting, structure, republication rules).
- **[[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]]** — organisation and independence of [[ANRE]].
- Trade register / company publicity: **Legea 265/2022** (ONRC).

## The state (who makes & applies law)
- **Parlamentul** — bicameral (Camera Deputaților + Senat); adopts laws (organic vs ordinary — Const. art. 73).
- **Președintele** — promulgates laws, issues decrees.
- **Guvernul** — hotărâri (HG, art. 108), ordonanțe and **ordonanțe de urgență** (art. 115).
- **Autonomous regulators** — e.g. **[[ANRE]]** (energy), BNR (central bank), ASF (financial markets), Consiliul Concurenței.
- **Agencies** — **[[ONRC]]** (trade register), **[[ANAF]]** (tax).
- **Judiciary** — ordinary courts; **Curtea Constituțională** outside that hierarchy.

## EU membership
Romania has been an **EU Member State since 1 January 2007**. EU law has primacy and direct effect within the constitutional framework (Const. art. 148). Energy acquis arrives as **binding EU law**, not via the Energy Community route that Moldova still uses. Cross-vault for instruments: `EU Law/`. Comparative MD notes: Moldovan Law vault (L164/L108/L10).
