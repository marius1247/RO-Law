# Concept — EU ETS arhitectura Phase 4

**EN:** EU ETS Phase 4 legislative architecture (Fit for 55) · **RO:** arhitectura legislativă EU ETS Faza 4

Cap-and-trade core: a declining annual emissions cap creates artificial scarcity; covered installations surrender **EU Allowances (EUAs)** equal to verified emissions.

Parent: [[EU ETS — sinteza pietei carbon]] · [[MOC — EU Carbon Pricing]]

---

## Linear Reduction Factor (LRF)

Annual rate at which the total allowance cap shrinks:

| Period | LRF | Target alignment |
|---|---:|---|
| 2024–2027 | **4.3%**/year | 62% net reduction by 2030 vs 2005 |
| 2028–2030 | **4.4%**/year | |

The LRF is the **primary long-run supply constraint** — more binding than auction calendar noise.

---

## Market Stability Reserve (MSR)

Supply-side buffer preventing allowance glut and price crashes.

| Trigger | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| TNAC > **833 million** | MSR **absorbs 24%** of excess allowances from upcoming auctions |
| TNAC < **400 million** | MSR **releases 100 million** allowances/year into auctions |
| Holdings > previous year's auction volume | Excess **permanently invalidated** |

TNAC (Total Number of Allowances in Circulation) published by the Commission each **May**.

> Open thread: Article 29a emergency price-release trigger (2.4× 2-year average over 6 months) — see [[Concept — EU ETS open threads]].

## Scope (ETS 1)

| Sector | Status |
|---|---|
| Power generation | Full coverage since 2005 |
| Heavy industry (steel, cement, chemicals, etc.) | Full coverage |
| Domestic aviation | Covered |
| Maritime transport | Phased in — **100% surrender from 2026** |
| Buildings / road transport | → separate **ETS 2** ([[Concept — ETS 1 vs ETS 2]]) |
