# Concept — ETS 2 pass-through si Social Climate Fund

**EN:** ETS 2 cost pass-through and Social Climate Fund · **RO:** transferul costurilor ETS 2 și Fondul Climatic Social

How upstream fuel distributors translate ETS 2 allowance costs into retail pump and heating prices, and how the **Social Climate Fund (SCF)** offsets regressive impact.

Parent: [[Concept — ETS 1 vs ETS 2]] · [[EU ETS — sinteza pietei carbon]]

---

## Pass-through chain

```
Upstream fuel supplier buys ETS 2 allowances
        ↓
Wholesale fuel price + CO₂ cost premium
        ↓
Retail consumer price (pump / gas bill)
```

ETS 2 regulates **fuel distributors** releasing fossil fuels (petrol, diesel, heating oil, natural gas, coal) into the market — not individual boilers or cars.

---

## Pass-through formula

```
ΔP_Retail = EF_Fuel × P_ETS2
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ΔP_Retail** | Retail price increase per litre or kWh |
| **EF_Fuel** | Emissions factor (tCO₂e per unit of fuel) |
| **P_ETS2** | Prevailing ETS 2 auction clearing price (€/tCO₂) |

Fuel distribution is a competitive, low-margin commodity industry → economists expect **near 100% pass-through**.

---

## Impact benchmarks

| Fuel | EF (approx.) | @ €45/t | @ €100/t |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Petrol | ~2.35 kg CO₂/l | +€0.106/l | +€0.235/l |
| Diesel | ~2.68 kg CO₂/l | +€0.121/l | +€0.268/l |
| Natural gas (heating) | ~0.20 kg CO₂/kWh | +€0.009/kWh | +€0.020/kWh |
| Heating oil | ~2.96 kg CO₂/l | +€0.133/l | +€0.296/l |

€45/t aligns with ETS 2 soft ceiling target (2020 real prices).

## Social Climate Fund (SCF)

**~€86.7 billion** mobilised across the EU (€65B from ETS 2 revenues + 25% Member State co-financing).

### Revenue flow
```
ETS 2 auction revenues
    ├── 25% → Social Climate Fund pool (+ MS co-financing)
    └── 75% → Member State budgets (100% earmarked for climate & social measures)
```

### Two-pillar offset framework

| Pillar | Mechanism | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **I — Structural** | Root-cause decarbonisation | Insulation · heat pumps · public transport vouchers · EV sharing |
| **II — Temporary income** | Immediate cushion | Cash transfers · energy bill credits for energy-poor households |

### Key design features
- **Pre-funding:** SCF disbursements begin **before** full ETS 2 compliance financial effect.
- **Targeted allocation:** weighted toward lower-income MS, rural areas, high energy/transport poverty (e.g. CEE).
- **100% earmarking:** remaining MS auction revenues must fund climate + social measures.

---

## Policy tension

Without SCF, ETS 2 is **regressive** — lower-income households spend a higher share of income on heating and transport with less capital for EVs or retrofits.
