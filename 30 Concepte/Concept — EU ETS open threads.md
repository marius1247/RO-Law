## 1. MSR mechanics & price containment

- TNAC calculation methodology (May release)
- 24% intake rate when TNAC > 833M
- Automatic invalidation above previous year's auction volume
- **Article 29a (ETS 1):** emergency release if EUA > 2.4× 2-year average for 6 months
- **Article 30h (ETS 2):** parallel price trigger for ETS 2 allowances

→ Foundation: [[Concept — EU ETS arhitectura Phase 4]]

## 2. Maritime & aviation sectoral nuances

### Maritime (ETS 1)
- Surrender phase-in: 40% (2024 emissions) · 70% (2025) · **100% (2026+)**
- Geographic scope: 100% intra-EU voyages · 50% extra-EU inbound/outbound
- **FuelEU Maritime** interaction — separate fuel-standard compliance layer

### Aviation
- Free-allocation phase-out by 2026
- SAF allowance provisions
- EU ETS vs global **CORSIA** overlap

## 3. Financial market structure & speculation

### COT & compliance calendar
- Weekly COT: Investment Funds (speculators) vs Commercial Undertakings (compliance)
- Surrender deadline shift: **April 30 → September 30** (from 2024) — alters March vs September futures rollover

### Options dynamics
- Put/call ratios · strike clustering (€80/t, €100/t)
- Hedging squeezes around December expiry (most liquid carbon contract globally)
