# Concept — Certificat verde

**RO:** certificat verde · **EN:** green certificate

## Definition
A tradable instrument issued for eligible electricity produced from renewable sources under **[[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|Legea 220/2008]]**. Obligated parties (chiefly suppliers) must acquire a statutory **quota** of certificates (or pay the substitute amount). The certificate is the unit of the support scheme — not a licence and not a connection right.

## How the scheme works
1. **Eligibility** — producer / technology / commissioning window qualifies under L220 and ANRE rules
2. **Issuance** — certificates credited for measured eligible production (subject to caps / banding historically)
3. **Obligation** — suppliers (and other obligated entities) must hold certificates equal to the annual quota
4. **Trading** — bilateral / organised market transfers
5. **Compliance** — surrender / reporting; substitute payment if short

ANRE methodologies below L220 set issuance, trading, and quota mechanics — Batch B ingest targets.

## Contrast with Moldova
| | RO L220 | MD L10/2016 |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | Tradable **green certificate** | Fixed tariff / competitive auction |
| Obligation | Quota on suppliers | Support paid to eligible producers |
| Main risk surface | GC price liquidity + connection | Connection + system integration |

## Interactions
- **State aid / scheme redesign** — GC parameters have been repeatedly adjusted by law and OUG; always check the consolidare year for the plant’s commissioning cohort
- **Connection** — a certificate does not create a right to inject; [[Concept — Racordare la rețea]] remains the physical gate
- **Licence** — generation / supply still need [[Concept — Licență în energetică]]

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|L220/2008]]
- [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] — market / producer interface
- ANRE Ordine on GC issuance, trading, quotas *(planned — [[Download checklist — Batch B ANRE]])*
