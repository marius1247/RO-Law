# Concept — Racordare la rețea

**RO:** racordare la rețea · **EN:** network connection

## Definition
The regulated procedure by which a specific installation — consumer premises, generating plant, storage — is **physically and contractually attached** to an electricity or gas network. Granted by the **network operator** under an ANRE procedure; it is not itself an ANRE licence decision. Appeal route typically runs operator → [[ANRE]] → court.

## Distinguish from licensing
| Gate | What it authorises |
|---|---|
| [[Concept — Licență în energetică\|Licence]] | Carrying on a *regulated activity* |
| **Connection** | Attaching a *specific installation* to a *specific network* |

A developer needs both, in that logical order. Projects die at connection far more often than at licensing.

## Typical stages *(electricity — Ord. 59/2013 lineage)*
1. Application / preliminary information from the operator
2. **Aviz tehnic de racordare (ATR)** — technical conditions and connection point
3. Design and construction of connection works
4. Connection contract and commissioning
5. Energisation / market entry for producers

Each stage carries **regulated maximum time limits** — the most practically useful content in the regulation.

## The three fights
- **Cost allocation** — deep vs shallow charging; how much reinforcement the applicant pays
- **Capacity and queue** — scarce capacity makes queue position the asset; speculative ATR holding is the pathology
- **Refusal** — only on grounds the regulation allows; unreasoned refusals are the standard ANRE complaint

- [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] — enabling articles (EE / gas titles)
- **ANRE Ordin 59/2013** (+ amendments) — electricity connection → [[ANRE — Regulament racordare EE 2013 (text)|Regulament racordare]] · [[ANRE — Regulament racordare EE 2013 (notă)|notă]]
