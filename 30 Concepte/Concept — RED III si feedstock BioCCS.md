# Concept — RED III si feedstock BioCCS

**EN:** RED III sustainability constraints on BioCCS feedstocks · **RO:** criterii RED III pentru biomasa BioCCS

Directive (EU) 2023/2413 fundamentally alters the BioCCS feedstock landscape — shifting from volume-driven harvesting to tightly audited **residue-based** supply chains.

Parent: [[Concept — DACCS vs BioCCS economics]] · CRCF gate: [[Concept — CRCF net removal accounting]]

---

## Three legislative constraints

### I. Cascading principle (Article 3(3))
Legal hierarchy for wood biomass:
```
Wood products → extend life → reuse → recycling → bioenergy → disposal
```
- Financial support **banned/restricted** for primary roundwood or industrial sawlogs burned for energy
- BioCCS cannot rely on whole trees / high-grade timber without losing state aid + CRCF eligibility

### II. Expanded "no-go" areas (Article 29)
Prohibited sourcing from:
- Primary and old-growth forests
- Highly biodiverse forests · heathlands · peatlands · high-carbon-stock soils
- Stump/root harvesting banned · clear-cutting restricted

### III. Lowered thresholds & GHG savings
- Sustainability criteria threshold lowered from **20 MW → 7.5 MW** thermal input
- GHG savings floor: **80%** (new plants) · **70%** (existing) vs fossil baseline
- Mandatory supply-chain mass-balance in Union Database

---

## Feedstock shift — eligible categories

| Category | Sources | Supply risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Secondary forest residues** | Sawmill offcuts, sawdust, bark, thinning waste | High demand from panel/pellet industries |
| **Industrial biogenic byproducts** | Black liquor (pulp/paper), fermentation CO₂ | **Ideal BioCCS target** — zero LUC risk |
| **Biogenic waste** | Sewage sludge, MSW wood fraction | Contamination · complex scrubbing |
| **Agricultural residues** | Straw, bagasse, stover, manure | Strict soil-organic-carbon balance (LULUCF) |

---

## Project design consequence

**Standalone greenfield BECCS power plants** fueled by imported wood pellets are largely **unviable** under RED III.

Instead: **retrofits on existing biogenic point sources:**
- Pulp & paper mills (black liquor recovery)
- Waste-to-energy (~50% biogenic fraction)
- Biogas / bioethanol (ultra-pure biogenic CO₂ off-gas)

---

## CRCF interaction

Biomass violating RED III criteria:
- Cannot be certified as permanent carbon removal under CRCF
- Risk of fossil-equivalent emissions accounting in EU ETS MRV — destroys project economics
