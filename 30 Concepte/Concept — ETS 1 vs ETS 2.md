# Concept — ETS 1 vs ETS 2

**EN:** EU ETS 1 vs ETS 2 structural comparison · **RO:** comparație structurală ETS 1 vs ETS 2

Two **separate, non-fungible** cap-and-trade markets under the revised EU ETS Directive.

Parent: [[EU ETS — sinteza pietei carbon]] · [[MOC — EU Carbon Pricing]]

---

## Direct comparison

| Dimension | ETS 1 (existing) | ETS 2 (from 2027) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sectors** | Power · heavy industry · maritime · aviation | Buildings · road transport · small industry |
| **Regulation point** | Downstream — direct emitters (~10,000 installations) | Upstream — fuel distributors |
| **Principle** | Polluter pays at smokestack/engine | Cost on fuel placed on market → passed to consumer |
| **Free allocation** | Phasing out via CBAM (2026–2034) | **100% auction** from day one |
| **Price control** | MSR intake/reinjection | Dedicated MSR + **~€45/t soft ceiling** (2020 real prices) |
| **Revenue recycling** | Innovation Fund · Modernisation Fund | **Social Climate Fund** (~25% of auction revenue) + MS climate budgets |
| **Status** | Operational since 2005 (Phase 4) | MRV active 2024–25; full compliance **2027–28** |

---

## Four structural differences

### 1. Downstream vs upstream enforcement
ETS 1: each installation tracks stack emissions and surrenders EUAs.
ETS 2: fuel suppliers surrender allowances based on fuel carbon content — administratively impossible to regulate millions of boilers and cars individually.

### 2. Zero free allocation in ETS 2
Buildings and road transport have **low carbon-leakage risk** (geographically fixed consumption) — no industrial competitiveness argument for free allowances.

### 3. Price controls & Social Climate Fund
ETS 2 has a front-loaded soft ceiling (~€45/t) releasing up to **200 million** allowances from reserve on price spikes. → [[Concept — ETS 2 pass-through si Social Climate Fund]]

### 4. Market ring-fencing
ETS 1 EUAs **cannot** satisfy ETS 2 obligations and vice versa. Prevents industrial demand from driving up household heating costs (and vice versa).
