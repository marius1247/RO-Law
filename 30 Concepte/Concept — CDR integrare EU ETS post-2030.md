# Concept — CDR integrare EU ETS post-2030

**EN:** carbon dioxide removal integration into EU ETS post-2030 · **RO:** integrarea eliminărilor de CO₂ în EU ETS post-2030

Transition from gross-emissions reduction to **net-zero / net-negative** balance sheets as the allowance cap approaches zero.

Parent: [[EU ETS — sinteza pietei carbon]] · accounting: [[Concept — CRCF net removal accounting]]

---

## Why CDR integration is required

```
Declining cap (LRF → ~zero late 2030s)     +     Residual hard-to-abate emissions
                    │                                        │
                    └──────────── CDR offsets 1:1 ────────────┘
```

Without negative emissions:
- **Residual deficit:** cement calcination, chemical synthesis, long-haul aviation, maritime — cannot be fully abated technically
- **Market illiquidity ("ETS endgame"):** near-zero cap → extreme volatility and supply crunches

---

## Regulatory bedrock — CRCF

Regulation (EU) 2024/3012 — **Carbon Removal Certification Framework**.

Removal units must satisfy **Qu.A.L.ITY** criteria:
- **Q**uantification · **A**dditionality · **L**ong-term storage · **s**ustainab**I**l**T**Y

**Permanence gate for ETS compliance:** only **permanent domestic removals** targeted:
- DACCS · BioCCS · Biochar Carbon Removal (BCR)
- Temporary nature-based removals (forestry, soil carbon) **excluded** from direct ETS compliance (reversal risk)

---

## Two integration models

### Model A — Centralised public procurement (Commission preference)
- Commission auctions dedicated EUA quantity (e.g. up to **250M allowances** 2030–2040)
- Auction revenues buy CRCF-certified permanent removals directly
- **Advantages:** preserves market integrity; bridges €200–500/t CDR vs €80–100/t EUA price gap

### Model B — Direct surrender / offsetting
- Installations purchase CRCF removal units and surrender 1:1 in lieu of EUAs
- **Safeguards required:**
  - Quantitative cap (e.g. max **5–10%** of compliance obligation)
  - Sectoral restraint (hard-to-abate only)

---

## Differential impact: ETS 1 vs ETS 2

| | ETS 1 | ETS 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **CDR role** | Direct offsetting for process emissions | Indirect / macro balancing |
| **Technology fit** | Point-source CCS · DACCS hubs · BioCCS networks | Expensive CDR → consumer fuel price spike risk |
| **Price sensitivity** | Industrial entities can absorb high-cost CDR with green product premiums | Highly price-sensitive upstream pass-through |

---

## Challenge matrix

| Challenge | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Price parity gap (DACCS €200–500/t vs EUA €80–100/t) | Innovation Fund · central procurement subsidies |
| Mitigation deterrence | Strict surrender caps · separate net-reduction targets |
| Reversal / liability | CRCF buffer reserves · operator liability rules |
