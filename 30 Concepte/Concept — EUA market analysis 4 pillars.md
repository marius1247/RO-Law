# Concept — EUA market analysis 4 pillars

**EN:** four-pillar EUA market analysis framework · **RO:** cadru de analiză a pieței EUA în patru piloni

Structured approach to European Union Allowance (EUA) price action. Parent: [[EU ETS — sinteza pietei carbon]] · index: [[_EU Carbon Market Analysis Index]].

---

## Pillar I — Fundamentals (supply vs demand)

### Supply
- Primary auction volumes (EEX, on behalf of Member States)
- MSR intake / reinjection figures (Commission, May)
- Free-allocation distribution to industrial benchmarks

### Demand
- Verified annual emissions (released **April** each year)
- Industrial output indices (steel, cement, chemicals PMI)
- Power-sector burn rates and electricity demand

→ Legislative supply: [[Concept — EU ETS arhitectura Phase 4]]

---

## Pillar II — Energy complex & fuel switching

Carbon prices correlate with the **marginal cost of dirty vs clean power**.

- **Fuel-switching price:** gas (TTF) vs coal (API2) with plant efficiency factors
- **Clean Spark Spread (CSS):** gas-fired profitability incl. EUA cost
- **Clean Dark Spread (CDS):** coal-fired profitability incl. EUA cost

When gas is cheap → coal burn falls → EUA demand drops. Gas spike → coal burn rises → EUA demand spikes.

→ [[Concept — Fuel switching price si clean spreads]]

## Pillar III — Political & policy risk

- EU 2040 target and post-2030 cap trajectories
- National interventions (coal phase-outs, carbon tax floors)
- MSR Article 29a emergency releases
- CBAM milestone years (especially 2029–2031 cliff)
- Speculative-trading intervention debates during energy crises

→ [[Concept — CBAM si phase-out alocari gratuite]] · [[Concept — Post-2030 ETS merger scenarios]]

## Pillar IV — Market structure & positioning

- **ICE / EEX order books:** Dec-expiry futures carry highest liquidity
- **COT (Commitment of Traders):** weekly net positions — Investment Funds vs Commercial Undertakings
- **Options open interest:** put/call ratios; strike clustering (e.g. €80/t, €100/t)
- **Compliance calendar:** surrender deadline moved to **30 September** (from 2024) — shifts futures rollover patterns (March vs September)
