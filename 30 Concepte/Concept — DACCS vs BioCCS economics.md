# Concept — DACCS vs BioCCS economics

**EN:** DACCS vs BioCCS cost, energy, and scalability comparison · **RO:** comparație economică DACCS vs BioCCS

Both deliver permanent negative emissions under CRCF, but operate on opposite sides of the energy-resource tradeoff.

Parent: [[Concept — CDR integrare EU ETS post-2030]] · feedstock rules: [[Concept — RED III si feedstock BioCCS]]

---

## Executive comparison

| Metric | DACCS | BioCCS / BECCS |
|---|---|---|
| **LCOE today (2025–26)** | €300 – €1,000+/tCO₂ (FOAK) | €130 – €250/tCO₂ (retrofits) |
| **LCOE target (2040–50)** | €150 – €300/tCO₂ (NOAK) | €100 – €180/tCO₂ |
| **CO₂ concentration** | ~0.04% (ambient air) | 8–15% (flue gas) |
| **Energy demand** | 1.5–2.5 MWh/tCO₂ | 0.4–0.8 MWh/tCO₂ |
| **Net energy** | Consumer (needs clean heat + power) | Producer or neutral |
| **Scaling bottleneck** | Renewable power · CapEx | Sustainable biomass (RED III) |
| **Siting** | High — co-locate over storage | Low — tied to biomass sources |

---

## Cost structure

### DACCS — high CapEx + high OpEx
- Massive air throughput at 420 ppm — fan arrays, sorbents/solvents
- OpEx: sorbent degradation + energy (40–60% of running costs)
- Thermal regeneration: 100°C (solid sorbents) to 900°C (liquid systems)
- **Cannot clear commercially** at current EUA prices — needs Innovation Fund or voluntary offtakes

### BioCCS — moderate CapEx, dual-revenue OpEx
- Established amine scrubbing on biogenic flue gas
- Revenue from power/heat/biofuels + removal credits
- **Economic parity** approaching at EUA > €110–140/t for many biogenic point sources

---

## Thermodynamics

Minimum work to separate CO₂ scales with concentration:

```
W_min = RT × ln(1/C_CO2)
```

- Ambient air (DACCS): 300× less concentrated → massive throughput penalty
- Flue gas (BioCCS): concentrated stream → low energy penalty

A 1 Mtpa DACCS plant may need **200–300 MW** continuous clean power.

---

## Scaling limits

| DACCS | BioCCS |
|---|---|
| Unlimited atmospheric feedstock · ~1–2 ha/Mt | Capped at ~100–150 MtCO₂/yr in Europe (biomass ceiling) |
| Bottleneck: grid-scale renewables | Bottleneck: RED III sustainability · LULUCF compliance |

## Policy timeline

| Period | Dominant technology |
|---|---|
| **2030–2035** | BioCCS on pulp/paper, WtE, bioethanol |
| **2035–2050** | DACCS as structural backstop once BioCCS hits biomass ceiling |
