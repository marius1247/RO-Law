# Concept — Societate pe acțiuni (SA)

**RO:** societate pe acțiuni (SA) · **EN:** joint-stock company

## Definition
The company form under **[[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|Legea 31/1990]]** whose capital is divided into **acțiuni** (shares — securities), designed for broader ownership, institutional governance, and (where applicable) public offering / listing. Liability of shareholders is limited to the amounts subscribed.

## Core features
| Feature | SA |
|---|---|
| Ownership unit | *acțiune* (security) |
| Transfer | Freer in principle; dematerialised / register mechanics |
| Organs | AGA + board (or dual structure options); heavier mandatory machinery for public SAs |
| Disclosure | Substantial for public companies |
| Register / overlay | [[ONRC]] + **ASF** if public / listed |

## When clients actually need an SA
- Sectoral law requires SA form (banks, insurers, certain utilities)
- Access to public capital markets
- Historic privatisation left the entity as SA
- Need for freely transferable securities and institutional board governance

Otherwise prefer [[Concept — Societate cu raspundere limitata|SRL]].

## Contrast with Moldova
MD SA law is standalone **L1134/1997** with an explicit **MDL 600,000** minimum and CNPF overlay. RO keeps SA inside L31 with ASF for the capital-markets layer. Map problems, not article numbers.

## Capital & governance flashpoints
- [[Concept — Capital social]] — minima, increases, reductions, net-assets duties
- Related-party transactions and minority protections
- Directors’ duties near insolvency

## Governing provisions
- [[Legea 31-1990 — societatile (text)|L31/1990]] — SA chapters
- Capital-markets legislation / ASF rulebook *(public SA)*
- Legea 265/2022 — [[ONRC]] registration
