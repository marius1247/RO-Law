# ANRE — Autoritatea Națională de Reglementare în Domeniul Energiei

**Level:** autonomous administrative authority / sector regulator — `forta_juridica` **8** acts in [[Ierarhia actelor normative|the hierarchy]]  
**Basis:** [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]] (organisation & independence) + sectoral mandates in [[Legea 123-2012 — energia electrica si gazele naturale (text)|L123/2012]] and [[Legea 220-2008 — promovare energie regenerabila (text)|L220/2008]].

## Mandate
Regulates electricity, natural gas, and thermal energy (as assigned). Core powers: **licensing**, **regulated tariffs and prices**, approving **network codes and market rules**, connection and supply regulations, quality-of-service standards, market monitoring, dispute handling, and interfaces to the **green-certificate** scheme.

Independence package (OUG 33/2007): legal personality, parliamentary control, own revenues (licence fees / contributions), decision-making autonomy from the Government — an **EU acquis requirement**, not a domestic ornament.

## Governance
Led by a president (and board structure as amended). Annual activity report to Parliament. Normative **Ordine** published in [[Monitorul Oficial]] Partea I.

## Pattern worth noting
Every operative duty should trace to an enabling article in L123/L220/OUG 33. Methodologies are normative; tariff *Decizii* applying them are individual. Challenge routes differ.
