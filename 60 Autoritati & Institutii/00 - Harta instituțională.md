# Harta instituțională (state governance map)

Who holds power and issues which acts. Grounded in the [[Constitutia Romaniei (text)|Constitution]] (esp. Titlul III) and ranked in [[Ierarhia actelor normative]].

## Legislative
- **Parlamentul României** — bicameral (Camera Deputaților + Senat); adopts organic and ordinary laws (art. 73)

## Head of state
- **Președintele României** — promulgates laws, issues decrees, foreign affairs & defence roles

## Executive
- **Guvernul** — HG (art. 108), ordonanțe, **[[Concept — Ordonanță de urgență|OUG]]** (art. 115)
- **Ministries** — sectoral normative acts (e.g. Ministerul Energiei, Ministerul Finanțelor, Ministerul Justiției)
- **Autonomous regulators** — [[ANRE]] (energy), BNR (central bank), ASF (financial markets), Consiliul Concurenței
- **Agencies** — [[ONRC]] (trade register), [[ANAF]] (tax)

## Judicial
- Ordinary courts: judecătorii → tribunale → curți de apel → **Înalta Curte de Casație și Justiție**
- **Curtea Constituțională** — constitutional review (outside the ordinary hierarchy)

## Territorial
- Local public administration (județe, municipii, orașe, comune) — local council decisions at the bottom of the force scale

## Profiles written
[[ANRE]] · [[ONRC]] · [[ANAF]]

## Why autonomous regulators sit apart
[[ANRE]], BNR, ASF and Consiliul Concurenței are insulated from day-to-day Government direction: fixed leadership rules, own or ring-fenced funding, accountability channels that are not pure ministerial hierarchy. Independence is also an **EU acquis requirement**. Worked energy example: [[OUG 33-2007 — organizarea ANRE (text)|OUG 33/2007]].
